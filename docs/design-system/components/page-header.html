Page header

The top of a page's content. An eyebrow, a light display title and one muted line that says what is here, with the page's actions to the side.

Overview

Every page that is more than a single card starts with a page header, directly under the top bar. It holds the page's one <h1>, a sentence of context that usually carries a live count, and the actions that apply to the whole page, such as New project. Things that belong to one item go on that item's card; the landing page uses a hero instead. A breadcrumb or a search field may sit inside it when the page needs one.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Contextoptional: an .eyebrow naming the area, or a breadcrumb on deeper pages.
  2. Title.page-head__title, the page's <h1> in the light display style.
  3. Sub.page-head__sub, one muted sentence at the large body size, often with a live count.
  4. Actions.page-head__actions, with .page-head--actions on the container: one primary button and up to two quiet ones.
  5. Slotoptional content below, such as a search form or a row of tags.

Sizes

one size. The header uses --space-16 above and --space-10 below, the h1 type style for the title and the large body style for the sub line.

States

The header itself is static. While the page's data loads, the title and sub become skeleton lines inside an aria-busy container and the actions stay usable; the eyebrow and breadcrumb are known and stay real text.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: everything stacks in one column; actions wrap below the sub line and the primary action may be .btn--block.
  • 768px: .page-head--actions moves the actions to the trailing edge, bottom-aligned with the text.
  • 1024px and up: the same, inside the page container; the title balances across lines and never exceeds the container.

Theming

Every colour reads a semantic role: the title is the default text role, the sub line muted and the eyebrow subtle. In dark mode they keep the same order of emphasis.

Accessibility

Role: plain content. The title is the page's only <h1>; the eyebrow is a <p>, not a heading. A search form inside it has role="search" and a label.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the breadcrumb links, the search field and the actions in reading order.

Focus: controls inside show the shared 2px --color-focus-ring. After navigation, move focus to main (or the <h1>) so the new title is announced first.

Labelling: the <title> element starts with the same words as the <h1>. A loading header keeps a polite status ("Loading projects") so the skeleton is not silent.

Motion: skeletons shimmer slowly; reduced motion stops the shimmer.

Touch: actions keep their 44px targets and wrap rather than shrink.

Content

Title the page with a noun people use: Projects, Notes, Field Day. Sentence case, no full stop. The sub line is one sentence with a real number: 148 notes, 12 edited this week. Don't restate the title ("Here are your projects") or explain the interface. The eyebrow names the area in one or two words: Workspace, Gathering.

Code

Use div.page-head as the first child of main inside .wrap. Add .page-head--actions when it has .page-head__actions.
