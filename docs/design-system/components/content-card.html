Content card

One course, module, video, article, collection or event in a grid or list. A tinted cover with the type's glyph, the type, the title, who made it, a few facts and, for a member, their progress.

Overview

The content card is how Logos Lab shows a piece of learning anywhere it is listed: the home page, Explore, search results, a topic, an expert's profile, a collection, the dashboard and Saved. Every type uses the same card so a learner reads them the same way; the type line and the cover glyph say what it is. Use the row variant in narrow lists and the dashboard. For people use the person list; for a dated listing of events use the event row.

Used in 2 mock states.

Anatomy

  1. Cardarticle.content-card, a birch surface with a hairline, 20px corners. The whole card is one link through the title.
  2. Cover__cover, a 16:9 tile in one of the muted tile tones (tile--sage, tile--clay, tile--fjord, tile--oat) with the type's line glyph (__glyph), or a photograph.
  3. Duration__duration, videos only; a small dark tag on the cover.
  4. Save__save, an icon button with aria-pressed, above the stretched link.
  5. Type__type, an overline with a small icon: Course, Module, Video, Article, Collection, Event.
  6. Title__title with the only link; its ::after stretches over the card.
  7. By__by, the expert's portrait and name, or the Logos Lab mark on an oat tile.
  8. Meta__meta, two or three facts: lessons, length, reading time, items, date.
  9. Progress__progress, a member's progress bar and a sentence; or __status with a badge.

Sizes

the grid sets the width (at least --layout-card-min); the row variant is the compact form.

States

Hover lifts the card 3px and strengthens its edge; focus on the title link rings the whole card. Saved fills the bookmark in the accent. In progress shows the progress bar and the next step. Completed shows a success badge. Unavailable (a saved item that was archived) dims the card, says so, and offers Remove.

Responsive behaviour

The grid fills as many 18rem columns as fit: one on a phone, two at 768px, three or four from 1024px. Row cards keep an 8rem cover (5rem under 30rem) beside the text. Titles wrap; nothing truncates.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: an article whose title heading holds the one link. The save button is a separate toggle with aria-pressed and a name that includes the title.

KeyEffect
TabMoves to the save button, then the title link.
EnterOpens the item, or toggles Saved on the button.
SpaceToggles Saved.

Focus: focusing the title link draws the ring around the whole card.

Labelling: the cover is decorative (aria-hidden glyph, alt="" photo). Progress bars carry aria-valuenow and a visible sentence, so the number is never colour alone.

Motion: the lift and the photo zoom stop under reduced motion.

Touch: the save button is a 44px target in the cover's corner, clear of the title link.

Content

Titles are the item's real title in title case as published: Imago Dei in an Age of Machines. Meta uses numerals and units: 24 lessons · 5 h 40 min, 12 min read, 9 items. Progress reads 42% · 10 of 24 lessons. Say who made it by name, never "by an expert".
