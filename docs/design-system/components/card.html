Card

Hold one person, project or gathering as a calm, rounded surface: a small square photo, a name, a few facts and one quiet action.

Overview

Use a card when each item in a collection is a thing in its own right — a person, a project, a gathering — and people compare items side by side in a grid. A card shows a summary and leads somewhere; it is never the whole record. Use a list when people scan many items in one column, a table when they compare the same fields across rows, and an aside card for a block of supporting facts beside the main content.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Mediaoptional 16:9 photo bleeding to the top edge (.card__media), for gatherings and places.
  2. Photo or initialsa 64px square with soft corners (.card__photo, or .card__initials on a .tile--* colour).
  3. Name and rolethe heading (.card__name) and one line of context (.card__role).
  4. Aside figureoptional number or short status at the top right (.card__aside), in the display face.
  5. Factstwo short label and value pairs between hairlines (.card__facts).
  6. Footerpresence or timing on the left, one quiet action on the right (.card__foot).

Sizes

cards take the width of their grid column; padding stays at --space-6 and the photo at 64px.

States

Hover lifts an interactive card by --lift and deepens its shadow and border; a static card has no hover of its own, only its action button does. Focus draws the 2px sage ring around the whole card. A selected card turns sage-tinted with an accent border. While data loads, a skeleton card of the same shape holds the space so the grid does not jump.

Responsive behaviour

At 360px cards stack in one column and the footer action may wrap under the status. From 768px .cards becomes two columns; from 1024px a page may set three columns with .grid. The photo stays 64px at every width and long names wrap rather than truncate.

Theming

Cards sit on --color-bg-surface with a hairline --color-border-default; in dark mode the surface lifts slightly above the canvas so cards still read as objects. Initials tiles use the --color-tile-* roles, which are tuned for both themes.

Accessibility

Role: an <article> labelled by its heading (aria-labelledby), inside a <ul> when cards form a collection. An aside card is an <aside> or a plain <section> with a heading.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the card's link or its action, in reading order.
EnterOn an interactive card, opens the record through the name link.
SpaceToggles the checkbox on a selectable card.

Focus: an interactive card shows one ring around the whole card when its stretched name link has focus; a static card focuses only its own action button.

Labelling: the photo and initials are decorative (alt="", aria-hidden) because the name sits beside them. Name every action after the person or thing: Message Grace, not Message. The selection checkbox needs its own label (aria-label="Add Hannah Kowalski to Field Day").

Motion: the hover lift is 1px over --duration-base; it is removed under reduced motion.

Touch: the whole interactive card is the target. Footer actions use btn--sm, 36px tall, above the 24px minimum.

Content

Lead with the name, then one line of role or context: Grace Liu — Designer. Keep facts to two pairs, each a short noun phrase: Working on — Harbour Notes. Write missing values as Not added yet, never a dash. Times are specific and local: Today, 9:40 am, Saturday 17 October 2026 · 10:00 am.

Code

Wrap collections in <ul class="cards"> and give each <article class="card"> an aria-labelledby that points at its .card__name. Add .card--interactive only when the name is a link and the card has no other controls.
