Form field

The wrapper that ties a label, a control, help text and an error together, so every question is understood the first time it is read.

Overview

A form field is the frame around one question: a visible label, the control, optional help, and the error message when the answer needs fixing. Every text field, textarea, select and group of checkboxes or radios sits inside a .field. Use form layout to arrange several fields, sections and the actions that submit them. A switch carries its own label row and does not need a .field.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Labela short noun in sentence case, always visible, linked with for / id.
  2. Optional marker(optional) in the label, in the subtle text role. Required fields carry no marker.
  3. Controlthe input, textarea, select or group of choices.
  4. Error messageappears after the control when the answer needs fixing, with an icon and a sentence that says what to do.
  5. Help textexplains why the question is asked or what format works; linked with aria-describedby.
  6. Countercharacters used against a soft limit, aligned to the end of the row.

Sizes

The field has one rhythm (an 8px gap between label, control, help and error). The control inside it sets the height; see the text field and select sizes.

States

The field itself has no states; it shows the state of its control. Hover darkens the control's border, focus draws the sage ring around the control, read-only fills it with linen and keeps the text strong, disabled fills it with linen and fades the text, and invalid turns the border and fill to the danger roles and inserts an error message linked with aria-describedby. Help text stays visible in every state so the reason for the question is never lost.

Responsive behaviour

Fields fill the width of their column at every size. Labels always sit above the control, never beside it, so long labels and translations wrap instead of squeezing the input. .field-row places two fields side by side from 48rem and stacks them below; keep a row to short answers (names, dates, postcodes).

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: no role of its own. A <label for> names a single control; a fieldset with a legend names a group.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to and from the control. Labels, help, errors and counters never take focus.
Click or tap on the labelFocuses the control, or toggles a checkbox or radio.

Focus: the ring sits on the control, never on the wrapper. After a failed submit, focus moves to the error summary (see form layout), whose links move focus to each field.

Labelling: every control has a visible label; placeholders are examples, never labels. Link help, error and counter with aria-describedby (error first) and set aria-invalid="true" on the control. A group's help is linked from the fieldset.

Motion: errors appear without animation; nothing shifts the label or the control.

Touch: the label extends the control's target. Controls are at least 44px tall at the default size.

Content

  • Labels are short nouns in sentence case with no colon: Project name, Meeting days, Email address.
  • Help says why or how, in one sentence: "Shown on the project card and in every invitation."
  • Errors say what to do next, not what went wrong: "Enter a project name", "Enter an email address like amara@example.com", "Keep the tagline to 60 characters or fewer". Never "Invalid input" or "This field is required".
  • Mark the few optional questions with (optional); ask only what you need.

Do and don’t

Tokens

The field reads the label, muted and subtle text roles, the danger foreground for errors and the 8px (--space-2) rhythm between its parts. .field-row uses the 24px gutter.

Code

Wrap each question in .field. Link help, error and counter ids from the control's aria-describedby, set aria-invalid="true" while the answer is wrong, and use fieldset.field with a legend.field__label for groups.

<div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="s336-ff-name">Project name</label><input class="input" id="s336-ff-name" name="s336-project-name" type="text" value="Northwind" autocomplete="off" aria-describedby="s336-ff-name-help"><p class="field__help" id="s336-ff-name-help">Shown on the project card and in every invitation.</p></div>
