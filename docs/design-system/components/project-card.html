Project card

A sample domain card for a piece of work in progress, kept as a reference. A letter mark, the name, who leads it, one line about it, a few facts and its stage.

Overview

This is a sample component. It comes from the original Banaro domain (people sharing products they are building) and ships as a worked example of a domain card, not as part of every system. An app should either prune it (audit_usage.py --prune removes it when no mock uses .project) or adapt it into its own card (a course, a listing, a case) by copying the block, renaming the classes (.project to .course, __mark, __facts and so on) and rewriting the facts. For a generic container use a card.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Carda surface with a hairline border and soft corners (.project) that lifts on hover.
  2. Marka 48px letter tile on a tile tone (__mark), decorative.
  3. Namethe project's name as a link (__name), the card's heading.
  4. Ownerwho leads it (__owner), subtle text.
  5. Lineone sentence about what it is (__line).
  6. Factsa short description list of label and value pairs (__facts).
  7. More rowwhen it last changed and its stage badge (__more).

Sizes

One size; project--compact is the denser form. Cards take the width of their grid column.

States

Hover darkens the hairline and lifts the card with --shadow-3 (and 3px of movement when motion is welcome). Focus lands on the name link; the forced state here rings the whole card the way :focus-within styling would. Loading shows a skeleton card of the same shape.

Responsive behaviour

One column at 360px, two from 768px and four from 1280px (.projects--list stops at three). Inside a card, facts keep label and value on one line, wrapping the value under itself when long; the more row wraps the badge below the date at 360px.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role. Tile tones darken in the dark theme and the mark's letter follows --color-tile-fg, so marks stay legible in both.

Accessibility

Role: each card is an <article> labelled by its name heading; the name is the only link, and the facts are a <dl>.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves from card name to card name.
EnterOpens the project.

Focus: the shared 2px --color-focus-ring outline on the name link.

Labelling: the letter mark is decorative (aria-hidden="true"). Stage badges are text, never colour alone.

Motion: the hover lift is removed under reduced motion; only the shadow and border change.

Touch: the name link is the target; keep cards at least 16px apart in the grid.

Content

The line says what the project is, in one sentence: A calm planning board for small teams. Facts are short nouns: Team: 4 people, Looking for: A designer. Update times are relative: Updated 2 days ago. Stages are one or two words in sentence case: Idea, Building, In beta, Paused.

Code

Wrap cards in .projects (add projects--list for long lists). Each .project holds __head (mark and name), __line, optional __facts and a __more row. When adapting, rename every project class and its @component project-card block together.
