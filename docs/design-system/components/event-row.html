Event row

A sample domain row for a dated gathering, kept as a reference. A large day number, the title, when and where, how many are going and one action; plus the matching header for the event's own page.

Overview

This is a sample component. It comes from the original Banaro domain (local gatherings) and ships as a worked example of a dated list row, not as part of every system. An app should either prune it (audit_usage.py --prune removes it when no mock uses .event) or adapt it for its own dated things (sessions, deliveries, appointments) by copying the block and renaming the classes. For an undated list use list rows; for a history of changes use a timeline.

Used in 2 mock states.

Anatomy

  1. Listul.events, rows divided by hairlines.
  2. Datea large display day over a small uppercase month (__date with __day and __mon), as a <time>.
  3. Titlethe event's name as a link (__title).
  4. Metawhen and where, each with a small icon (__meta).
  5. Sidehow many are going (__going) and one action, on the right from 1024px (__side).
  6. Event headeron the event's own page, .event-head pairs a date chip (__date) with the title and actions.

Sizes

One size per form: the row in lists, .event-head on the event's page. The day number always uses the --font-size-3xl display size.

States

Focus rings the title link. Going swaps the count and action for a success status ("You're going") and a quiet way to change it. Full says so and offers the waitlist. Cancelled strikes through the title, greys the day and replaces the action with a badge. Past greys the date and title and offers the notes instead of a reservation.

Responsive behaviour

.events is a size container, so the row follows the width of its list rather than the screen. Below 40rem (every phone, and narrow columns such as an aside) the date sits in a narrow left column and the title, meta and side stack to its right, with the action under the meta. From 40rem (a full page column from 768px up) the row becomes three columns: date, details and the side, with the count and action aligned right. .event-head stacks the date chip above the title at every width.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: ul.events with one li.event per gathering. The date is a <time datetime>; the title is a heading containing the only link to the event.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves from the title link to the row's action, then to the next row.
EnterOpens the event or activates the action.

Focus: the shared 2px --color-focus-ring outline on the title link and the action.

Labelling: the meta repeats the weekday and time in full, so the big day number is not the only date. Icons are decorative. Make actions specific when several rows share them ("Reserve a spot for Field Day" as aria-label on a short visible label). Status is text ("You're going", "Full", "Cancelled"); the strike-through on cancelled titles is backed by the badge.

Motion: no motion of its own.

Touch: row actions are 36px small buttons with 12px between them; keep a whole row at least 64px tall.

Content

Titles are the event's name: Field Day. Meta lines are short: Sat 17 Oct, 10:00 am – 2:00 pm, Riverside Park, north lawn. Counts read 18 going · 12 spots left. Say why a gathering was cancelled in a few words when you can: Called off because of the storm forecast.

Do and don’t

Rows read the canvas, hairline and text roles, the display type for the day, --size-event-date for the date column, --color-accent-subtle and --color-fg-accent for the header's date chip, and the status roles for badges.

Code

Use ul.events of li.event: __date (a <time> with __day and __mon), __main (title and meta) and __side. Add is-cancelled or is-past as needed. When adapting, rename the event classes and the @component event-row block together.
