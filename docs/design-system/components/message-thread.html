Message thread

A private conversation between people. Soft bubbles, theirs on the left and yours on the right in sage, with a labelled reply box beneath.

Overview

Use a message thread for a private conversation between two or a few people, read and written in turn. Pair it with an inbox that lists the conversations. For public replies under a piece of work use comments; for a one-way announcement use a notification item or an alert.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Threadthe surface card (.thread) that holds the log and the reply box.
  2. Day dividera centred caption between hairlines (__day): Today, Wed 7 Oct.
  3. Their bubblelinen (.bubble), aligned left, at most 34rem wide.
  4. Your bubblesage wash (.bubble--mine), aligned right.
  5. Metatime, and Read or Sending… for your last message (__meta).
  6. Reply boxa labelled textarea and a Send button (__composer).

Sizes

One size. Bubbles size to their words up to 34rem, and the thread fills its column.

States

Focus rings the Send button. Sending adds your bubble at once with a dashed outline and "Sending…"; the reply box empties and stays usable. Failed turns that bubble to the danger wash and offers Try again in the meta. Empty is a new conversation: one line says who it is with, and the reply box is ready.

Responsive behaviour

At 360px bubbles take up to 85% of the thread so both sides stay distinct, and the Send button stays beside the reply box; the thread is a size container, and only when it is narrower than 18rem does Send drop below the box. At 768px a thread usually sits beside its inbox in a split view; at 1024px and up bubbles stop at 34rem so lines stay readable. The log scrolls; the reply box stays at the bottom of the view.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a <section> named "Conversation with …"; the messages sit in a role="log" region so new messages are announced politely. The reply box is a <form>.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between the reply box, Send and any Try again button.
EnterIn the reply box, adds a line break (Ctrl+Enter or the Send button sends).
Enter, SpaceOn Send or Try again: activates it.

Focus: after sending, focus stays in the emptied reply box. Try again moves focus back to the reply box.

Labelling: alignment and colour are not enough to tell who spoke: every bubble's meta starts with a visually hidden "You," or the sender's name, and group bubbles show the name in text. The reply box's label names the recipient ("Message Daniel Reyes"); the placeholder is never the only label. Failures use role="alert", and a sending bubble sets aria-busy="true".

Motion: new bubbles appear without sliding under reduced motion; the spinner stops.

Touch: Send is a 44px pill; Try again is at least 36px tall.

Content

Times are short (9:06 am) with a day divider above each day (Today, Wed 7 Oct). Only your last message says Read or Sending…. Explain what cannot be done and why in one sentence: Peter Walsh closed his account. You can read this conversation but not reply. The empty line invites a first message: Start a conversation with Ruth.

Code

Wrap the messages in .thread__log with role="log". Add bubble--mine to your messages, bubble--sending while one is in flight and bubble--failed when it fails. Put the reply box in .thread__composer.
