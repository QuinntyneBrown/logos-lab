Sign-in card

The calm, centred card for signing in, creating an account and the steps around them: one title, a few fields and one way forward.

Overview

Use the sign-in card for every step of getting into Logos Lab: sign in, create an account, reset a password and the "check your email" pause between them. It sits alone in the middle of an otherwise quiet page, with nothing to distract from the one task. Each card has one title, one primary action and one link to the neighbouring step. For forms inside the product, use a form layout instead.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Carda surface up to 30rem wide with an extra-large radius, centred on the page (.auth__card).
  2. Titlethe step, in the display size (.auth__title); it is the page's only h1.
  3. Subtitleone sentence of reassurance or instruction (.auth__sub).
  4. Formlabelled fields, a help link and one full-width primary button.
  5. Alternativea single line that links to the neighbouring step (.auth__alt).

Sizes

one size. The card is at most --size-auth-card (30rem) wide; its padding grows from --space-8 by --space-6 on phones to --space-12 from 640px.

States

Focus shows the sage ring on the field. Invalid marks each field that needs fixing with a message beneath it, and a form with more than one problem lists them first. Submitting turns the primary button into a busy button with a spinner and a present-tense label, and the fields stay as typed. Error puts a danger alert at the top of the card when the server says no, such as a wrong password or an email that already has an account.

Responsive behaviour

At 360px the card fills the width less the page margins and its padding tightens to --space-8 by --space-6; the primary button is full width at every size. From 640px the card is centred at up to 30rem with --space-12 of padding. The page around it keeps only the brand and a footer link; no navigation competes with the form.

Theming

The card sits on --color-bg-surface against the canvas with a hairline border. In dark mode the surface is a step lighter than the canvas, so the card still reads as one object; the alert uses the danger roles, tuned for both themes.

Accessibility

Role: a <section> labelled by the title, which is the page's single h1, containing a <form> labelled by the same title. Each field has a visible <label>.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the fields, the help link, the button and the alternative link.
EnterSubmits the form from any field.
SpaceToggles the checkbox and activates buttons.

Focus: on load, focus goes to the first field. After an invalid submit it moves to the form summary (or to the only invalid field); after a server error it moves to the alert.

Labelling: fields use the right type and autocomplete (email, current-password, new-password, name) so password managers and autofill work, and pasting into password fields is never blocked (WCAG 3.3.8). Offer the show-password toggle from the text field on longer forms. A busy button keeps a readable label (Signing in…) and sets aria-busy.

Motion: only the spinner moves while submitting; it stops under reduced motion and the label carries the meaning.

Touch: fields are 52px tall and the primary button is btn--lg; links have at least --space-3 around them.

Content

Title each step with what people are doing: Sign in, Create your account, Reset your password, Check your email. Keep the subtitle to one warm, specific sentence. Say password rules before people type, and failures without blame: That didn't match, not Invalid credentials. Never say which of email or password was wrong when signing in.

Code

Centre the card with .auth inside the page's main column. Put the title and subtitle in one wrapper so the card's --space-8 gap separates them from the form, not from each other.

<div class="auth"><section class="auth__card" aria-labelledby="s1323-auth-in">
  <div><h3 class="auth__title" id="s1323-auth-in">Sign in</h3><p class="auth__sub">Welcome back. Your notes are where you left them.</p></div>
  <form class="form" action="#" novalidate aria-labelledby="s1323-auth-in">
    <div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="s1323-in-email">Email</label><input class="input" id="s1323-in-email" name="s1323-email" type="email" autocomplete="email" required></div>
    <div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="s1323-in-pass">Password</label><input class="input" id="s1323-in-pass" name="s1323-password" type="password" autocomplete="current-password" required></div>
    <p class="field__help"><a href="#">Forgot your password?</a></p>
    <button class="btn btn--primary btn--lg btn--block" type="submit">Sign in</button>
  </form>
  <p class="auth__alt">New to Logos Lab? <a href="#">Create an account</a></p>
</section></div>
