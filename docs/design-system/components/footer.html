Footer

The quiet end of every page. A hairline, a line about who makes the product, a few muted links and one line of fine print.

Overview

The footer closes every public page and most product pages. It holds what people look for last: who makes the product, the legal and help links, and one line of fine print. It is deliberately small and muted, with lots of space above it, so a page ends calmly instead of with a second navigation bar. Don't repeat the top bar here, and don't put actions or sign-up forms in it; those belong on the page.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Footer<footer class="footer">: the section gap above, a hairline, then generous padding.
  2. Made line.footer__made, who makes the product and the copyright, in the body-small muted style.
  3. Linksnav[aria-label="Footer"] > ul.footer__links, muted links that wrap as a row.
  4. Fine print.footer__land, one or two sentences in the subtle role at a wide measure: a data promise, an acknowledgement, a registered address.

Footer · minimal

Sizes

one size. The footer sits --layout-section-gap below the content, with --space-16 above and --space-24 below its contents; text uses --text-body-sm and the body size for links.

States

Links rest in the muted text role with no underline. Hover lifts them to the default text role and adds the underline; keyboard focus draws the sage ring. The made line and fine print are static text.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: everything stacks: made line, links wrapping in a row with --space-6 between them, then the fine print.
  • 768px: the same stack, with the links on one line.
  • 1024px and up: .footer__grid becomes two columns, the made line beside the links, and the fine print spans both columns at --layout-measure-wide.

Theming

Every colour reads a semantic role: the hairline is the default border, links are muted, fine print is subtle. In dark mode they keep the same order of emphasis on the charcoal canvas.

Accessibility

Role: <footer> (the contentinfo landmark when it is a direct child of <body>) containing a <nav aria-label="Footer"> of plain links.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the footer links in reading order.
EnterFollows the focused link.

Focus: the shared 2px --color-focus-ring, offset 3px, on each link.

Labelling: name the nav "Footer" so it is distinct from the "Primary" nav in the top bar. Link text names the destination; never "Click here".

Motion: only the shared link colour transition; nothing to reduce.

Touch: links wrap with --space-2 rows and --space-6 columns between them; on touch screens give each link at least a 24px target.

Content

Keep the made line to one sentence: who makes it and the copyright, © 2026 Logos Lab. Link labels are one or two words: Privacy, Help, Status. The fine print is plain and specific, a promise or a fact, never marketing. Never put a newsletter form or a wall of social icons here.
