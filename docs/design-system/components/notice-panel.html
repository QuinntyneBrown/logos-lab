Notice panel

The notifications popover from the top bar's bell: a head with Mark all as read, the list of notifications, and a link to notification settings. It becomes a full-screen sheet under 640px.

Overview

Logos Lab has no notifications page: the bell in the top bar opens this panel. It holds the member's notifications as a notification list, newest first, with unread ones marked. On a phone it fills the screen. For a short-lived confirmation use a toast; for a small piece of help beside a control use a popover.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Paneldiv.notice-panel, a raised card anchored under the bell: absolutely positioned below the top bar at the right margin, --size-notice-panel wide, never taller than the viewport. The specimens on this page add style="position: static" only so the panel shows in flow.
  2. Head__head, the title (__title) and a Mark all as read text button.
  3. Body__body, scrolls on its own and holds a .notice-list.
  4. Foot__foot, a link to Notification settings.

Responsive behaviour

From 640px the panel hangs under the bell at the right page margin, --size-notice-panel wide, and its body scrolls when the list is longer than the viewport. Under 640px it is a full-screen sheet with no corners or border, and the head carries a Close button so the sheet can be dismissed by touch.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Notification settings

Accessibility

Role: a non-modal dialog named "Notifications", opened by the bell button (aria-expanded, aria-controls). On a phone it is modal. The list is a ul of notification items.

KeyEffect
Enter or Space on the bellOpens the panel and moves focus to its title.
TabMoves through Mark all as read, each notification link and any actions, then Notification settings.
EscapeCloses the panel and returns focus to the bell.

Focus: focus goes to the panel's title on open and back to the bell on close. Clicking outside closes it.

Labelling: unread rows begin with visually hidden "Unread:". The bell's name includes the count ("Notifications, 3 unread").

Motion: the panel settles in from just above; under reduced motion it appears in place.

Touch: every row is a 44px target through its link; the full-screen sheet keeps the close button in reach at the top.
