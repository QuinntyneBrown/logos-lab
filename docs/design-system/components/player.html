Player

The frame for a video or a learning module: a dark media stage with play, captions and controls for video, and a light stage with a status bar for SCORM modules. It stands in for the Tessera video-player and scorm-player.

Overview

Logos Lab plays videos with the Tessera video-player and learning modules with the Tessera scorm-player. This component is the frame around them in the mocks and the design system: it fixes the stage, the overlays, the controls and the module bar so the embedded players look like Logos Lab. The video stage is dark in both themes so the picture keeps its contrast. Use player--module for SCORM modules. Pair a video with its transcript; place either inside the lesson layout.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Playerdiv.player, a rounded frame on --color-bg-media with on-media text.
  2. Screen__screen, the 16:9 picture area; a poster img fills it when there is one.
  3. Play__play, a large round sage button in the centre before playback starts.
  4. Overlay__overlay, a scrim over the screen with a title (__overlay-title), a sentence and actions (__overlay-actions): resume, error or sign in.
  5. Captions__captions, one or two lines on a scrim near the bottom of the screen.
  6. Controls__controls, a row of round __btn buttons, the time (__time), the scrub slider (__scrub with __scrub-fill) and a spacer (__spacer).
  7. Module bar__bar, for player--module: the module title (__bar-title), the save status, Exit and Full screen.
  8. Stage__stage, the light area that holds the SCORM package.

Sizes

the player fills its column at 16:9 (16:10 for modules); player--fill removes the frame for full-width and full-screen use.

States

Hover deepens the play button and focus rings it in the media accent. Loading dims the screen and shows a spinner. Resume offers to continue from the saved point. Error explains that the video cannot play and offers to try again. Sign in covers a members-only video for a visitor.

Responsive behaviour

The screen keeps 16:9 at every width. Under 640px the time is hidden to give the scrub room; the buttons stay 44px. The module stage drops its fixed ratio under 640px and grows with its content; the bar wraps so Exit and Full screen move below the title. player--fill takes the whole viewport in full screen.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a group of native buttons; the scrub is role="slider" with aria-valuetext in minutes and seconds. Captions is a toggle with aria-pressed. Settings opens a menu. Overlays that report a failure use role="alert".

KeyEffect
Space or KPlays or pauses.
Left / Right arrow on the scrubMoves back or forward 5 seconds.
Home / End on the scrubGoes to the start or the end.
CTurns captions on or off.
FEnters or leaves full screen.
EscapeLeaves full screen, or exits a module after confirming.

Focus: controls use the media accent ring so it shows on the dark stage. Focus never lands on the picture itself.

Labelling: every icon button has a name ("Play", "Captions", "Settings, speed 1.25×"). The play button names the video. The save status is a polite role="status".

Motion: the play button's grow on hover stops under reduced motion. Nothing autoplays.

Touch: every control is a 44px target; the scrub has an invisible 12px band above and below it.

Content

Times are m:ss or h:mm:ss: 12:34 / 24:18. Overlay titles are short questions or plain facts: Pick up where you left off?, This video won’t play right now. Module status reads Progress saved, Saving… or Not saved — retrying. The module title is the module's catalogue title.
