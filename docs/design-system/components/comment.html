Comment

A short, signed reply under a piece of work. Portrait, name, when, then the words, separated from the next comment by a hairline.

Standard componentContrast verifiedUnused in mocks

Overview

Use comments for public replies attached to one thing: an update on Northwind, a page in Harbour Notes. Each comment is signed and dated, and the list reads top to bottom, oldest first. For a private back-and-forth between people use a message thread; for system events (someone joined, a date changed) use a timeline or a notification item.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Portraita 40px square avatar, or initials on a tile when there is no photo. Decorative: the name sits beside it.
  2. Namethe author (__name), a link to their profile.
  3. Metawhen, and whether it was edited (__meta), as a <time>.
  4. Bodythe comment itself (__body), muted text at a readable measure.
  5. Actionsoptional text buttons (__actions): Edit and Delete on your own comments, Reply on others'.

Sizes

One size. Comments follow the body type and a 40px portrait; a denser list is a list, not a smaller comment.

States

New marks comments that arrived since the person last looked, with the accent wash and a New pill. Sending shows the viewer's own comment straight away in the subtle text colour with "Sending…" in the meta, so typing never feels lost. Failed keeps the words, explains in the danger colour and offers Try again and Delete. Deleted leaves a quiet placeholder so replies still make sense.

Responsive behaviour

The portrait stays beside the text at every width; the name, time and New pill wrap onto two lines at 360px. The body never grows wider than the wide reading measure, so long comments stay readable on a 1024px screen and up. Action buttons wrap rather than shrink.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a <ul class="comments"> named for what it belongs to ("3 comments on the Northwind update"), one <li class="comment"> per comment. Times are <time datetime>.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the author links and comment actions in reading order.
Enter, SpaceActivates Reply, Edit, Delete or Try again.

Focus: the shared sage ring on author links and text buttons. After Delete, focus moves to the next comment's first control, or to the comment box if none is left.

Labelling: portraits are decorative (alt="") because the name sits beside them. Action labels name the person where it helps ("Reply to Grace"). Your own comments say "(you)" in text. The New pill is real text, not only colour. A sending comment sets aria-busy="true"; a failure is announced with role="alert".

Motion: the spinner and the New wash do not animate under reduced motion.

Touch: text buttons are at least 36px tall; author names are links with a full line-height target.

Content

Times are relative for the last day (Today, 9:12 am, Yesterday, 5:40 pm) and dates after that (Wed 7 Oct). Write errors as what happened and what to do: We couldn't post this. Check your connection. Keep action labels to one or two words: Reply, Edit, Delete, Try again.

Code

Use ul.comments with one li.comment each: a portrait, then a div holding __head, __body and an optional __actions row. Add comment--new for unread comments and comment--failed when posting fails.
