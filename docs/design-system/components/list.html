List

A single column of items separated by hairlines, from plain links to rich rows with a photo, two lines of text and an action.

Overview

Use a list when people read items one after another: notebooks in a sidebar, members of a project, the next few things to do. .list is for short items — usually links; .rows is for richer items with a photo, a title, a line of detail and an action. Use a table when people compare the same fields across items, and cards when each item needs room for a photo and several facts.

Used in 1 mock state.

Anatomy

  1. Itemone <li>, separated from the next by a hairline (.list > li or .rows > li).
  2. Leading visualoptional icon, photo (.avatar) or initials tile.
  3. Titlea link or plain text; in rows, the h4-sized .rows__title.
  4. Metaone muted line of detail under the title (.rows__meta).
  5. Trailing slotoptional status, badge or one small action at the end of the row.

Sizes

list items are at least --target-comfortable (44px) tall; rows add --space-4 above and below their content.

States

Hover changes a link's colour (and underlines a row title); focus draws the sage ring on the link. Current marks the open page or the item shown beside the list in sage. Loading shows skeleton rows of the same shape, and empty replaces the list with a short message and the action that fills it.

Responsive behaviour

Lists are single-column at every width. At 360px row meta wraps under the title, and a trailing badge or button stays on the right while the text column shrinks (min-width: 0). From 768px rows may show a second trailing item, such as a date. In a split view from 1024px, the list pane marks the open item as current.

Theming

Lists inherit the surface they sit on and draw hairlines in --color-border-default. The current item uses --color-accent-subtle with --color-fg-accent text, tuned for 4.5:1 in both themes.

Accessibility

Role: a native <ul> with an aria-label (or a visible heading) that names the collection. Navigation lists sit in a <nav> with its own label. Row titles are headings when the list is a main section of the page.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through each item's link and action in order.
EnterFollows the focused link.

Focus: every link and action shows the sage ring. Don't make a whole row clickable around other controls; one link per row plus an optional action keeps focus order clear.

Labelling: photos and initials are decorative (alt="", aria-hidden) because the name follows. Give repeated actions a hidden suffix (Open Northwind). Mark the current page with aria-current="page", not only with colour. A loading list sets aria-busy="true".

Motion: none besides the shared colour transitions; skeletons stop shimmering under reduced motion.

Touch: links are at least 44px tall; small row actions are 36px and sit --space-4 from the badge.

Content

Lead each item with the name people look for: Harbour Notes, Grace Liu. Meta lines join two or three short facts with a middle dot: Designer · Lisbon. Order lists by what people need first — most recent, most used or alphabetical — and say which in the heading when it is not obvious.

Do and don’t

Tokens

Lists read the hairline border, the default, muted and accent text roles, --color-accent-subtle for the current item and --target-comfortable for row height.

--space-3
--border-width-hairline
--color-border-default
--target-comfortable
--color-accent-subtle
--space-4
--text-h4
--text-body-sm
--color-fg-muted
--radius-md
--color-fg-accent
--font-weight-semibold

Use <ul class="list"> for short items and <ul class="rows"> for rich rows built from an optional visual, .rows__main (title and meta) and a trailing slot.
