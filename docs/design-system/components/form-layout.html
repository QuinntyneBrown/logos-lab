Form layout

Arrange fields into a calm, single-column form — sections, side-by-side pairs, an error summary and one clear submit action.

Overview

Form layout is how form fields become a form: one column, generous 24px gaps, related questions grouped into sections, and a single row of actions at the end. Use .form for a plain stack (in a page or a dialog), .form-card to set a long form on its own surface with sections, and .form-inline for a one-line form such as inviting a person. When a submit fails, put a .form-summary at the top that links to every problem.

Used in 3 mock states.

Anatomy

  1. Form.form: a grid with 24px between fields. .form-card adds a surface, hairline and padding.
  2. Sectionfieldset.form-section with a legend.form-section__title, for a group of related questions.
  3. Field row.field-row: two short fields side by side from 48rem.
  4. Error summary.form-summary: a danger panel listing each problem as a link to its field.
  5. Actions.form-actions: the submit button and its quiet alternative; --end aligns them right.

Sizes

Forms take the width of their container, capped at the 44rem narrow measure on wide pages. Spacing is fixed: 24px between fields, 32px between sections, 40px of card padding from 40rem.

States

A submitting form keeps every answer visible, sets the submit button busy with a spinner and a present-tense label ("Creating project…"), and keeps Cancel available. An invalid form shows the error summary at the top, moves focus to it and marks each field with its own message; answers are never cleared. A saved form confirms next to the actions with a success message, or moves on to the next page.

Responsive behaviour

Forms are one column at every width; only .field-row pairs short fields from 48rem. .form-card padding grows from 24px to 40px at 40rem. .form-inline wraps its fields and button onto new lines when there is no room. On phones, .form-actions wraps and the primary button may become btn--block at the bottom of a sheet or dialog.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a native <form>; give it an accessible name (aria-label or aria-labelledby the page heading) when a page has more than one. Sections are fieldset / legend.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields in visual order, then to the actions.
EnterIn a single-line field, submits the form through its submit button.
Activating a summary linkMoves focus to the field with the problem.

Focus: after a failed submit, focus moves to the error summary (tabindex="-1", role="alert"); its links move focus to each field. After success, focus moves to the confirmation or the next page's heading. Never move focus while someone is typing.

Labelling: every field has a visible label; the summary lists the same messages as the fields, each as a link to its control. A busy submit button keeps its accessible name and sets aria-busy="true".

Motion: nothing animates except the button spinner, which stops under reduced motion.

Touch: actions keep 44px targets with a 12px gap; the primary action never sits next to a destructive one.

Content

  • Ask only what you need, in the order people think about it.
  • Section titles are short nouns: About Field Day, Who can join.
  • The summary title counts the problems: Fix 2 problems to continue; each item repeats the field's error.
  • The submit button names the outcome: Create project, Send invitation, Publish gathering — never "Submit". While busy: Creating project…. Afterwards: Changes saved.

Code

Use form.form (or .form-card with fieldset.form-sections) around your .fields, end with .form-actions, and render .form-summary first when a submit fails, then focus it.
