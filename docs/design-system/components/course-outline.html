Course outline

A course's sections and lessons in order, each lesson marked done, current or locked, with its length. It sits on the course page and beside every lesson.

Overview

The course outline lists a course's sections and their lessons so a learner can see where they are, what they have finished and what comes next. It appears on the course page, in the aside of the lesson layout from 1024px, and in the Outline drawer below that. Each section is a native disclosure. For a flat list of steps in a form flow use the stepper; to reorder lessons in the studio use the sortable list.

Used in 1 mock state.

Anatomy

  1. Outlinediv.outline, a stack of sections divided by hairlines.
  2. Sectiondetails.outline__section with a summary holding the section name (__name), a count (__count) and a plus or minus sign drawn by CSS.
  3. Lessonsol.outline__lessons, the section's lessons in order.
  4. Lessona.outline__lesson, or a span when locked: a mark, the title and the length.
  5. Mark__mark, a round 24px badge: a tick when done, a lock when locked, empty otherwise.
  6. Time__time, the lesson length in tabular numerals.

The full outline on a course page: open sections show their lessons, closed ones only their name and count.

Sizes

one size; outline--compact is the denser form for the aside and drawer.

States

Hover washes an available lesson in --color-bg-subtle. Focus draws the shared ring around the lesson row. Current (aria-current="page") fills the row with the sage wash, rings the mark in the accent and sets the title in semibold. Done (.is-done) fills the mark with the success tick. Locked (.is-locked, a span with aria-disabled) dashes the mark, shows a lock and turns the text subtle; it is not a link.

Responsive behaviour

The outline fills its container at every width. Titles wrap and the time stays on the right; the summary keeps the count beside the name until it runs out of room. Below 1024px it moves out of the lesson aside into the Outline drawer; on the course page it sits in the main column at every width.

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: native details and summary for each section and an ordered list of links for the lessons. The current lesson has aria-current="page". A locked lesson is a span with aria-disabled="true", so it is skipped by Tab but still read in the list.

KeyEffect
TabMoves through section summaries and available lessons in order.
Enter or Space on a summaryOpens or closes the section.
Enter on a lessonOpens the lesson.

Focus: the shared ring sits on the summary or the lesson row. Opening the lesson page puts the current lesson in view in the aside.

Labelling: state is never the mark alone: each done, current or locked lesson carries visually hidden text (", completed", ", current lesson", ", locked: finish the previous lesson first"). The count says "1 of 4 done" in words.

Motion: none; sections open instantly.

Touch: every row is at least 44px tall and the whole row is the target.

Content

Number sections and lessons as the course does: 3. Image and likeness, 3.2 Made, not manufactured. Times are whole minutes: 12 min. The count reads 1 of 4 done, or 4 lessons before a learner starts. Explain a lock in words: finish the previous lesson first.
