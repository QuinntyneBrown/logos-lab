Radio group

Choose exactly one answer from a few visible options — as calm cards with a sentence each, or as a plain list.

Core componentContrast verified

Overview

Use a radio group when people must choose exactly one of two to six options and benefit from seeing them all at once: who can open a notebook, when to be reminded, how to sort. With more options, or options people already know by heart, use a select. To switch a view in place, use a segmented button group. For any number of answers use checkboxes; for a single on/off setting use a switch. Pre-select the safest sensible option whenever one exists.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Groupfieldset.choices with a legend that asks the question.
  2. Option cardlabel.choice: hairline border on the surface; sage border and wash when chosen.
  3. Radiothe native radio in the accent colour, 20px.
  4. Title.choice__title, the answer in a few words.
  5. Descriptionoptional .choice__text, one sentence on what the answer means.

Sizes

One size. Cards grow with their text; plain and compact rows keep a 44px target.

States

Hover strengthens the card border (or tints the title in the plain list). Focus draws the sage ring on the radio. Checked moves the sage border and wash to the chosen card — in the matrix, the selection moves from the first option to the second. Disabled fills cards with linen and greys the text. Invalid outlines every card in the danger colour and shows the error under the question, with no option chosen.

Responsive behaviour

Cards stack in one column at every width. .choices--inline fits as many 10rem columns as the row allows and wraps the rest, so three short options sit side by side on a tablet and stack on a 360px phone. Keep inline titles to three words.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: native radios sharing one name, inside a fieldset whose legend names the group (radiogroup).

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the group (to the checked radio, or the first) and out of it. The group is one tab stop.
Down / RightMoves to and selects the next option, wrapping at the end.
Up / LeftMoves to and selects the previous option.
SpaceSelects the focused option if none is selected.

Focus: the ring sits on the radio; the card's sage border shows which option is chosen, the ring shows where the keyboard is.

Labelling: each card is a label wrapping its radio, title and description, so the whole card selects and the description is read with the title. The legend asks the question. Errors are linked from the fieldset with aria-describedby.

Motion: border and wash changes use 240ms colour transitions only.

Touch: the whole card or row is the target, at least 44px tall.

Content

  • The legend is a question or a clear noun: Who can open Harbour Notes?, Sort comments.
  • Titles are short and parallel: Just me, Northwind team, Anyone with the link.
  • Descriptions state the consequence in one sentence: "Read only. Good for sharing with guests."
  • Include a real "none" option (No reminder) instead of leaving the group empty.

Code

Use fieldset.choices with a legend, then one label.choice per option holding the radio, .choice__title and an optional .choice__text. Add .choices--inline or .choices--plain for the other layouts.
