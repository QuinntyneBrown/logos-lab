Badge and status

Label the state of a thing in two or three words, or tag it with a topic. Quiet tinted pills; the word always carries the meaning.

Overview

A badge (.badge) tells people the state of something: Draft, In review, Published, Overdue. It has a small dot and a tone. A tag (.pill) names a topic or category — Research, Design systems — and has no dot. Neither is interactive; a badge never doubles as a button. To let people filter by a topic, use a chip. For a sentence about what happened, use an inline message or an alert.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Dota small circle in the text colour; it marks a status badge and is hidden on count and plain badges.
  2. Labelone to three words in sentence case, --text-caption.
  3. Containera fully rounded tinted pill, 24px tall for badges and 28px for tags.

Sizes

badges and tags have one size each (24px and 28px), sized to sit on a line of body text. In dense tables they keep the same size; the row grows instead.

States

Badges and tags are passive and have one state. The tone is the state of the thing they describe, so choose the variant, never a hover or selected look. A removable tag's remove button follows the button states; once removed, the tag leaves the list and focus moves to the next tag, or to the input that adds tags.

Responsive behaviour

Badges never wrap inside themselves; long labels belong in text, not in a badge. A .tags list wraps onto new lines at 360px and keeps an 8px gap. Inside a table row at narrow widths, a badge moves under the title rather than shrinking.

Theming

Each tone pairs a *-bg and *-fg role that both change in dark mode, so a badge stays at 4.5:1 on either theme. The solid and count badges read the accent pair, which turns light sage with dark text in dark mode.

Accessibility

Role: plain text in a <span>; a list of tags is a <ul class="tags"> with an aria-label. When a badge changes while the page is open (Draft → Published), announce the change in a separate role="status" message; do not make the badge itself a live region.

Focus: badges and tags are not focus stops. A removable tag's button is, and shows the shared focus ring.

Labelling: the word is the meaning — the colour and dot only repeat it. A count badge includes the noun in a .vh span ("3 unread notes"). A remove button names the tag: "Remove topic Harbour Notes".

Motion: none. A badge that changes tone swaps instantly.

Touch: the remove button keeps at least the 24px minimum target inside the pill.

Content

Use one to three words in sentence case, describing the state of the thing, not an instruction: In review, Due Friday, Overdue by 2 days. Tags are nouns: Research, Field Day. Do not end with punctuation, do not use all caps, and do not invent new tone meanings — warning always means "soon", danger always means "late, failed or blocked".

Code

Use .badge with one tone modifier, or .pill (plus .pill--accent) inside .tags for topics. Count badges use .badge--count with a .vh noun.
