Feature area

Show the few places a product offers as numbered, quiet cards that each lead somewhere.

Overview

Use feature areas to introduce the two to four places a product is built around — on a landing page below the hero, or on a welcome screen as next steps. Each card is one link with a number, an icon, a title, one sentence and a "go" cue. For a list of records (projects, people), use a card grid or a person list; for a single invitation, use a grain panel.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Indexa two-digit caption ("01") that gives the set a gentle order.
  2. Icon tileoptional 48px rounded square in --color-accent-subtle holding a 24px line icon.
  3. Titlean h3 naming the place in two or three words.
  4. Textone muted sentence on what you do there.
  5. Go cuea link-coloured label with a trailing arrow that nudges on hover.
  6. Containerthe whole card is one <a>: surface, hairline border, --radius-lg, lifting 3px on hover.

Sizes

one content-driven size. Cards share a row height because each list item is a grid cell; the container width decides the column count.

States

Hover lifts the card 3px, deepens its shadow, darkens the border to --color-border-strong and nudges the arrow. Keyboard focus draws the sage ring around the whole card. Feature areas are always links, so they have no disabled or selected state: remove an area you cannot offer rather than greying it out.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: one column; cards stack at full width with a 16px gap.
  • 768px: two columns. Four areas form a two-by-two block.
  • 1280px and up: four columns for the default set; areas--two stays at two columns so a pair never floats in a half-empty row. Text wraps inside each card; titles never truncate.

Theming

Cards read --color-bg-surface and --color-border-default; the icon tile reads --color-accent-subtle with --color-fg-accent. In dark mode the tile becomes a deep sage with a light sage glyph and the hover shadow is replaced by the darker elevation scale, so the lift stays visible.

Accessibility

Role: a <ul> of links. Each card is a single <a> whose accessible name is its title, text and go cue in reading order; the title stays an h3 so screen-reader users can jump between areas by heading.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves from card to card; each card is one stop.
EnterOpens the area.

Focus: the 2px --color-focus-ring, offset 3px, follows the card's rounded corners.

Labelling: icons are decorative (aria-hidden="true"). The index is visible text; keep it short so the announced name starts quickly. Never put a second link or button inside a card.

Motion: the lift and the arrow nudge run only when motion is welcome. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, hover changes only the border and shadow.

Touch: the whole card is the target, at least 200px tall on phones.

Content

  • Titles name a place, not a feature: Projects, Notes, Gatherings. Two or three words.
  • The sentence says what you do there, in under 15 words: "Plan, track and ship the work that matters, one project at a time."
  • The go cue is a specific verb: Open notes, Plan a gathering. Avoid "Learn more" on every card.
  • Keep the index to two digits, or one or two words ("Next up") for a single card.
