Sortable

The course and collection builder's reorderable list: sections holding lessons, each with a drag handle and Move up and Move down buttons, so order can be changed by mouse, touch or keyboard.

Overview

In the studio, experts order a course's sections and lessons and a collection's items with the sortable list. Dragging is a shortcut: every move can also be made with the Move up and Move down buttons or with the keyboard on the handle, and every move is announced. For a fixed, read-only list of lessons use the course outline; for tabular data use a table.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Groupdiv.sortable__group, a sunken panel for one section: a head (__group-head) with the section title (__group-title) and Add lesson, then its list.
  2. Listol.sortable, the items in order.
  3. Itemli.sortable__item, a birch row: handle, main text and actions.
  4. Handle__handle, an icon button with a grip; drag it, or press Space on it to pick up and arrows to move.
  5. Main__main, the title (__title) and meta (__meta): type and length.
  6. Actions__actions, Move up and Move down icon buttons (and Remove where allowed).
  7. Drop lineli.sortable__drop, the 4px accent line where a dragged item will land.

Sizes

rows are content height with 44px controls; the list fills its column.

States

Dragging lifts the picked-up item with the sage wash, an accent border and a shadow. Drop target shows the accent drop line where the item will land. Invalid marks a section that cannot be saved, such as an empty one, with a danger wash and a message in its head.

      Responsive behaviour

      Under 480px each item's actions move below its title, beside the handle's column. Drag works with touch at every size; the Move buttons are always there as the reliable alternative. Groups keep 16px padding.

      Theming

      Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

      Accessibility

      Role: an ordered list of items; each handle is a button. Picking up an item with the keyboard sets aria-pressed="true" on its handle. A polite live region announces each move ("3.3 The image restored in Christ, position 2 of 3") and the drop.

      KeyEffect
      Space or Enter on a handlePicks the item up, or drops it.
      Up / Down arrow while picked upMoves the item one place; across a section boundary it moves to the next section.
      Escape while picked upPuts the item back where it was.
      Enter on Move up / Move downMoves the item one place.

      Focus: after a move, focus stays on the same item's handle or button in its new place.

      Labelling: handles and Move buttons include the item's title ("Move 3.2 Representing, not resembling up"). Move up is disabled on the first item and Move down on the last.

      Motion: the lift and the slide of neighbouring items stop under reduced motion; items swap in place.

      Touch: a long press on the handle starts a drag; handles and Move buttons are at least 44px.

      Content

      Titles carry their number in courses (3.2 Representing, not resembling) and their real title in collections. Meta is the type and length: Article · 14 min, Video · 24:18. The empty-section message says what to do: Add at least one lesson, or remove this section.
