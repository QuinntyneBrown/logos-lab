Top bar

The bar across the top of every page. Brand on the left, a few quiet links, the person's actions on the right, and one hairline beneath.

Overview

The top bar is the one piece of navigation on every page: the brand that links home, three to five primary destinations, and the actions that belong to the person, such as sign in, messages or their account. It sits on the page canvas with no fill, separated by a single hairline. Keep it short: section navigation belongs in tabs or a sidebar navigation, and page actions belong in the page header. Below 64rem the links fold into a Menu button that opens them as a sheet.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Barheader.header around .header__inner: 72px tall, aligned to the page container by .wrap (on either element), with a hairline bottom border.
  2. Brandthe brand lockup, first after the skip link.
  3. Primary navigationnav.nav with .nav__link items; the current page gets a small sage dot beneath its label.
  4. Actions.header__actions, pushed to the trailing edge: text and primary buttons when signed out; icon buttons with counts and the account button when signed in.
  5. Menu button.menu-btn, shown below 64rem; it opens the navigation as a popover sheet.

Sizes

one size. The bar is --layout-topbar-height (72px) at every width; nav links are 44px tall at desktop and in the sheet, and every action keeps a 44px target.

States

The matrix shows the primary links, where the bar's own states live. A link is muted at rest; hover lifts it to the default text role (and adds a linen wash in the mobile sheet); keyboard focus draws the sage ring. The current page keeps the default text role and gains a 4px sage dot beneath its label, with aria-current="page". Icon buttons, the account button and the Menu button follow button: a linen wash on hover and the shared ring on focus. When the sheet is open, the page behind takes the backdrop.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: brand, the primary call to action or the account button, and an icon-only Menu button (the label hides below 30rem). Sign in, .header__link and the nav links move into the sheet. A .header__search wraps to a full-width second row.
  • 768px: the same bar, with the Menu button's label visible and the search back on the first row.
  • 1024px and up: the nav links sit inline after the brand, Sign in and .header__link appear, the account button shows the first name, and the Menu button and the sheet's Close button disappear. .header--compact keeps the Menu button and the sheet at every width.

The sheet opens from the top as a popover with --shadow-4 over --color-bg-backdrop, inset --space-3 from the viewport edges.

Theming

The bar has no fill; it takes the page canvas and the default border role, so it needs no dark-mode rules. In dark mode the current-page dot and the primary button turn light sage, and the sheet uses the raised charcoal surface.

Accessibility

Role: <header> (the banner landmark when it is a direct child of <body>) containing a <nav aria-label="Primary"> of plain links. The Menu button is a native <button popovertarget>, which exposes aria-expanded for free; the sheet is the same <nav> with the popover attribute, so there is one list of links, not two.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the skip link, brand, nav links and actions in visual order.
EnterFollows a link or activates a button.
Enter, Space (Menu button)Opens the navigation sheet; focus stays on the button and Tab moves into the links.
EscapeCloses the open sheet (light dismiss) and returns focus to the Menu button.

Focus: every link and button shows the 2px --color-focus-ring, offset 3px. Nothing in the bar is position: sticky by default; if a product makes it sticky, give main a matching scroll-margin-top.

Labelling: name the nav "Primary". Icon buttons carry their count in the name ("Notifications, 3 unread") and hide the visual badge with aria-hidden. The account button names the person ("Your account, Amara Osei") and announces aria-haspopup="menu". Mark the current link with aria-current="page", never with colour alone.

Motion: link colours change over 240ms and the sheet settles in over 640ms; reduced motion removes both.

Touch: every link and action is at least 44px tall. The sheet's Close menu button spans its full width.

Content

Use nouns for destinations, in sentence case and one or two words: Projects, Notes, Gatherings. Keep to five links. Signed out, pair Sign in with one invitation: Get started, never two primary buttons. A .header__link states something new and specific: 2 new replies, not "Activity". Use the first name on the account button: Amara.

Code

Put .wrap on the <header> or on .header__inner; both align the bar to the page container. One <nav class="nav" popover> serves both layouts: inline at 64rem and up, a sheet below. Point the Menu button and the sheet's Close button at its id with popovertarget. Add .header--compact to keep the sheet at every width and .header__search to a .search form to give it the flexible slot.
