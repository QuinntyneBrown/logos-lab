Grain panel

The signature invitation: a warm clay panel with a whisper of wood grain that asks for one considered next step.

Overview

The grain panel is the Banaro look in one component: a clay-tinted surface (--color-bg-panel) with large rounded corners and a barely-there wood grain, holding a short invitation and one primary action. Use it for the one moment on a screen that deserves a pause: turning on a digest, saving a seat, inviting the team. The grain is decorative and used at most once per screen; a second panel on the same screen drops it. For a routine message use an alert or a card; for the page's opening promise use the hero.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Panel.panel: --color-bg-panel, --radius-xl, generous padding (48px, 80px by 64px from 1024px).
  2. Grain.grain: a ::before layer of fractal-noise wood grain in --color-grain, masked and tiled at 900 × 420px, at 9% opacity in light and 6% in dark. It never carries meaning.
  3. Eyebrowoptional overline naming the feature.
  4. Title.panel__title, a short question or invitation in the h2 role, about 16 characters a line.
  5. Text.panel__text, one or two muted sentences that say exactly what happens next.
  6. Actions.panel__actions: one primary lg button and an optional .panel__note of social proof.
  7. Example listoptional .panel__list of .panel__item rows (40px photo, text, .panel__meta) that shows what the invitation leads to.

The full panel: the invitation on one side, a short example list on the other. At 1024px and up the two sides sit in a 1.2fr / 1fr grid with an 80px gap.

Sizes

Two sizes by modifier: the default panel (48px by 24px padding, 80px by 64px from 1024px) for a full invitation with an example list, and panel--compact (32px by 24px, 40px from 1024px) for text and actions only. The panel always fills its container's width.

States

The panel is static; its action carries the states. Hover deepens the primary pill, focus draws the sage ring, and each .panel__item lifts 3px with a soft shadow under the pointer when motion is welcome. While the action is working, the button shows its own busy state; the panel never dims.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: one column with 24px side padding. The example list follows the actions; actions wrap and the note drops under the button.
  • 768px: still one column, with the title on two lines and wider text.
  • 1024px and up: the invitation and the list sit side by side (1.2fr / 1fr), vertically centred, with 80px between them. The grain tile stays 900 × 420px at every width, so it never stretches or blurs.

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-panel (clay 100 in light, night 850 in dark) and the grain reads --color-grain at --grain-opacity: clay 500 at 9% in light, clay 300 at 6% in dark. Example items sit on --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1. Adjust the grain only through those tokens; never paint it with an image or a raw colour.

Accessibility

Role: a <section> labelled by the panel title. The grain is a CSS pseudo-element with pointer-events: none, so it is invisible to assistive technology and never intercepts a click.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the primary action, then any secondary action. Example items are not focusable.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused action.

Focus: the shared 2px --color-focus-ring stays above 3:1 on the clay panel in both themes.

Labelling: example portraits use alt="" because the name is written beside them. The title states the offer, so the button can stay short.

Motion: the example items' 3px lift runs only under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. The grain never moves.

Touch: lg actions are 52px tall; the note is plain text, not a target.

Content

  • The title is a question or an invitation, under 40 characters: "Start the week knowing what moved."
  • The text says what happens and when: "Every Monday at 8:00 am, …". No exclamation marks.
  • The action names the outcome: Turn on the digest, Save my seat.
  • The note is one honest fact of social proof: "Read by 1,240 teams". Leave it out rather than invent one.

Code

Put .grain on the panel only when it is the single signature moment on the screen. The panel creates its own stacking context (isolation: isolate), so the grain stays behind the content without a z-index on the children.

<section class="panel grain" aria-labelledby="s1910-digest-title">
  <div class="panel__grid">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">Weekly digest</p>
      <h3 class="panel__title" id="s1910-digest-title">Start the week knowing what moved.</h3>
      <p class="panel__text">Every Monday at 8:00 am, Logos Lab gathers the three changes in Northwind worth your attention, and nothing more.</p>
      <div class="panel__actions">
        <button class="btn btn--primary btn--lg" type="button">Turn on the digest</button>
        <span class="panel__note">Read by 1,240 teams</span>
