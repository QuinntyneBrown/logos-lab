Progress bar

Show how far along a task is when you can measure it. A thin sage line on a hairline track, with the words above it.

Overview

Use a progress bar for work with a known size — an upload, an import, a setup checklist, a profile that is 80% complete. When you cannot measure the work, use the indeterminate bar for a region or a spinner for a control. For steps people move through themselves, use a stepper. Never show a bar for work that finishes in under a second.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Labelwhat is happening, in body-small text above the track (.progress-label).
  2. Valuethe percentage or "Step 2 of 4", right-aligned in tabular figures.
  3. Trackan 8px pill in --color-border-default.
  4. Fill.progress__bar in --color-accent, as wide as --ll-progress-value.

Sizes

one 8px track. A taller bar does not make progress clearer; put the bar near what it measures instead.

States

Default shows the measured value and animates the fill to each new width over 420ms. Loading (indeterminate) is for the moment before the size is known: aria-busy="true" removes the value, and a short fill lifts off the start of the track and drifts across it until the first real value arrives. Success and danger are outcomes, shown as variants above.

Responsive behaviour

The bar always fills its container. At 360px a long label wraps and the value stays on the right of the first line. From 768px, keep bars inside cards or form columns; never stretch one across a wide page.

Theming

The track reads --color-border-default and the fill --color-accent (or the success and danger solid roles), each with a dark value; in dark mode the fill is light sage on a charcoal track. The fill keeps 3:1 against the track and the surface.

Accessibility

Role: role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax. Use aria-valuetext when the number alone is unclear ("Step 2 of 4: invite your team"). An indeterminate bar leaves out aria-valuenow and sets aria-busy="true".

Focus: a progress bar is not a focus stop.

Labelling: name it with aria-labelledby pointing at the visible label, or aria-label for a bare bar. Do not announce every percent; announce the start and the result in a role="status" message ("Upload complete. 12 photos added.").

Motion: the fill eases to each new width and the indeterminate fill drifts across the track over 3.6 seconds; under reduced motion the width jumps and the indeterminate fill rests in the middle of the track, and the label still says what is happening.

Touch: N/A: a progress bar has no target. Any cancel or retry button beside it keeps 36px.

Content

The label says what is happening, with the object: Uploading field-day-photos.zip, Importing 128 notes. Values are whole percentages (64%) or steps (Step 2 of 4). When it finishes, say the result (Upload complete, 12 photos); when it stops, say how much was kept (8 of 12 photos are uploaded).

Code

Set style="--ll-progress-value: 64%" on .progress together with aria-valuenow="64", and keep a .progress__bar child. Put a .progress-label above it with an id for aria-labelledby.

<div><div class="progress-label"><span id="s1756-pd-label">Uploading field-day-photos.zip</span><span class="num">64%</span></div><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-labelledby="s1756-pd-label" aria-valuenow="64" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="--ll-progress-value: 64%"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div></div>
