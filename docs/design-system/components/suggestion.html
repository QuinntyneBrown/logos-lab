Suggestion

A sample domain card that suggests a person to meet, kept as a reference. Who they are, a plain reason why, and two calm choices.

Overview

This is a sample component. It comes from the original Banaro domain (suggesting people to work with) and ships as a worked example of a recommendation card, not as part of every system. An app should either prune it (audit_usage.py --prune removes it when no mock uses .suggest) or adapt it for its own recommendations (a course to take next, a note to revisit) by copying the block and renaming the classes. Keep the reason box whatever you adapt it to: a suggestion without a "why" feels like a guess. For a plain list of people use a person list.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Carda surface with a hairline border (.suggest); linen when suggestions are paused (--paused).
  2. Persona 48px portrait, the name as a link and one line of context (__who, __name, __meta).
  3. Scoreoptional: how strong the fit is, in the display face (__score).
  4. Reasona linen box that says in one or two sentences why (__why).
  5. Actionsone primary choice and one quiet pass (__actions).

Sizes

One size. Cards fill their grid column; use .btn--sm for the actions so two fit on one line at 360px.

States

Focus rings the primary action. Loading keeps the label and adds a spinner while the introduction is sent. Accepted replaces the choices with a success line and a link to the new conversation. Passed collapses the card to one quiet line with Undo, so a mis-tap is easy to take back.

Responsive behaviour

At 360px the portrait, name and score share one row and the two actions sit side by side below the reason. From 768px suggestions sit two to a row in a grid; at 1024px and up, three. The reason box always spans the card.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: each suggestion is an <article> labelled by the person's name; a paused panel is a <section> labelled by its title.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabMoves from the name link to the two choices.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused choice.

Focus: after accepting or passing, focus moves to the new status line's action (Open the conversation, or Undo).

Labelling: the portrait is decorative (alt=""); the name heading names the card. The score is text ("92% fit"). Results use role="status" so they are announced without stealing focus. Choice labels say what happens: Introduce yourself, Not now.

Motion: the passed card collapses without animation under reduced motion.

Touch: small 36px pills with 12px between them; never put the two choices closer.

Content

The reason starts with "Why" and the first name, then one or two plain, specific sentences drawn from what the two people share: Why Daniel. You both wrote about offline sync in Harbour Notes. Never show raw signals ("Overlap score 0.87"). Passing is gentle: Not now, and the result says what it means: We won't suggest him again.

Code

Use .suggest with __who (portrait, name and meta, optional __score), __why and __actions. Add suggest--paused for the paused panel and the passed line. When adapting, rename the suggest classes and the @component suggestion block together.
