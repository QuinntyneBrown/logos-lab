Workflow bar

The sticky bar at the top of every studio editor: what is being edited, where it is in the publishing workflow, whether it is saved, and the next action.

Overview

Every editor in the Logos Lab studio (course, article, collection, event) sits under the workflow bar. It names the item, shows its state with a badge, reports autosave, shows the path from Draft to Published, and holds the one or two actions that move the item on. Experts see Submit for review; editors see Request changes and Approve. For a page's static title and actions outside the studio use the page header; for a multi-step form use the stepper.

Used in 1 mock state.

Anatomy

  1. Bardiv.workflow, sticky to the top of the editor on the canvas, with a hairline below.
  2. Inner__inner, the page container's width; wraps its parts.
  3. Title__title, the item's name (__name, one line, truncated) over the meta row (__meta).
  4. Statea badge in the meta: Draft, In review, Changes requested, Approved, Scheduled, Published.
  5. Autosavean inline status in the meta: "Saved 10:24 am", "Saving…", "Not saved — retrying".
  6. Stepsol.workflow__steps, Draft › In review › Approved › Scheduled › Published; done steps are muted, the current one is accent and semibold.
  7. Actions__actions, Preview and the next workflow action, primary last.

Sizes

one size; small buttons keep the bar short.

States

Hover, focus and loading are the primary action's own: loading shows the spinner while the item is submitted. Saving replaces the saved time with a spinner and "Saving…". Not saved turns the status warning-coloured and says it is retrying; the actions stay available and the edits stay on the page.

Responsive behaviour

At 360px the title takes the full row, the steps wrap below it, and the actions wrap last; the name truncates with an ellipsis and its full text is in the editor's title field. From 768px the steps sit beside the title. From 1024px everything fits on one row for most titles.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a plain region at the top of the editor; the steps are an ordered list named "Publishing workflow" with aria-current="step" on the current one. The autosave status is a polite role="status".

KeyEffect
TabMoves to Preview, then the workflow actions.
Enter or SpaceRuns the action; Submit for review opens a confirmation dialog.

Focus: after an action the focus returns to the bar's primary action, now showing the next step.

Labelling: the state is written in the badge and the current step, never only the step's colour. Autosave announces "Saving…" once per save, not on every keystroke.

Motion: the spinner stops under reduced motion and the text alone carries the state.

Touch: buttons are small pills but keep a 44px target through their padding and spacing.

Content

States are single words or short phrases in sentence case: Draft, In review, Changes requested, Approved, Scheduled, Published. Autosave reads Saved 10:24 am, Saving…, Not saved — retrying. Actions name the workflow step: Submit for review, Request changes, Approve, Schedule, Publish now.
