Chart

A single-series chart in the one sage hue, for the analytics an expert or editor reads: a column chart over time or horizontal bars across categories, each with the same numbers as a table.

Standard componentContrast verifiedUnused in mocks

Overview

Logos Lab analytics are small, honest charts: one measure, one hue, a title that says what is being counted, and the same numbers as a table one click away. Use columns for a measure over time (weekly completions) and bars for a measure across named categories (how far learners reach in a course, how much of a video is watched). Never compare two series in one chart; draw two charts. For a single number use a stat; for one person's progress use a progress bar.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Chartsection.chart, the head, the plot and the data twin.
  2. Head__head, the title (__title) and a note (__note) giving the period and the count of learners.
  3. Plot__plot, a row of columns over a strong baseline. Height is --size-chart-height.
  4. Column__col, focusable, named in words, with its value as --ll-chart-value (a percentage of the plot's maximum).
  5. Bar__bar, the sage fill. The one series colour.
  6. Label__label, the value over selected columns only: the highest and the latest.
  7. Tip__tip, a dark tooltip with the exact value on hover or focus.
  8. Axisol.chart__axis, the column labels under the baseline.
  9. Rowsol.chart__rows of __row (label __row-label, track __track with its bar, value __row-value) for horizontal bars.
  10. Datadetails.chart__data, the same numbers in a table.

Weekly completions of a course's lessons. Labels sit on the highest week and the latest full week; every other value is in the tip and the table.

Sizes

columns share the chart's width; the plot height is --size-chart-height. Bar rows are content height.

States

Hover or focus on a column deepens its bar, hides its label and shows the tip with the exact value. Focus also draws the ring. Loading (aria-busy="true" on the chart) greys the bars and, unless motion is reduced, shimmers them. Suppressed: when fewer than 5 learners are in the group, the chart is replaced by a sentence so no one can be identified; the table is not offered either.

Responsive behaviour

Columns share the width at every size; at 360px the axis shows every label in caption type, and the chart keeps all eight weeks rather than scrolling. Bar rows put the label above the bar under 480px and beside it from 480px. The table twin scrolls inside its table-wrap if it must.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a section named by its title. Each column is a focusable role="img" named in words ("Week of 5 Oct: 41 completions"); the tip, labels and axis are hidden from screen readers because the name already carries them. Bar rows are a list whose label and value are text. The table twin is the full accessible version.

KeyEffect
TabMoves column by column through the plot, then to Show the numbers as a table.
Enter or Space on the summaryOpens or closes the table.

Focus: the ring draws around the column and the tip appears above it, so a keyboard user sees the same value as a mouse user.

Labelling: the title says what is counted and the note says over what and for whom. Values are never colour-coded; there is only one series.

Motion: the shimmer while loading and the hover fade stop under reduced motion. In forced colours the bars draw in CanvasText.

Touch: tapping a column shows its tip; the table is the comfortable way to read exact numbers on a phone.

Content

Titles name the measure in sentence case: Lesson completions per week, Learners who reached each section, How much viewers watched. Notes give the subject and the base: Imago Dei in an Age of Machines · 212 enrolled. Weeks are Week of 5 Oct. Percentages are whole numbers. The suppression sentence is always the same: Fewer than 5 learners are in this group, so we don’t show their numbers.
