Transcript

A video's words as timed cues under the player. Each time seeks the video, the cue being spoken is highlighted, and a search marks every match.

Overview

Every Logos Lab video has a transcript under the player. It lets a learner read instead of watch, find a passage, and jump the video to it. The cue being spoken follows playback. Use it only with a player; for an article's text use prose, and for a long list of results use the search and filter toolbar.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Transcriptdiv.transcript, the search field above the list.
  2. Searcha text field of type="search" labelled "Search the transcript".
  3. Listol.transcript__list, scrolls on its own up to --size-transcript-max.
  4. Cueli.transcript__cue, a time and a sentence or two.
  5. Time__time, a link-coloured button that seeks the video to that cue.
  6. Text__text, the words in muted small text; the current cue turns default.
  7. Hitmark.transcript__hit, a warm highlight on each search match.

Sizes

the transcript fills its column; the list height caps at --size-transcript-max and scrolls.

States

Hover underlines a time and focus rings it. Current (aria-current="true" on the cue) washes the cue in sage and darkens its text. Search marks each match and says how many there are. No matches replaces the list with a sentence and keeps the query.

Responsive behaviour

Times keep their column and the text wraps beside them at every width. On a phone the list sits below the player; from 1024px a lesson may place it beside the player at the same height.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: an ordered list of cues; each time is a native button. The current cue has aria-current="true". The match count is a polite role="status".

KeyEffect
TabMoves to the search field, then through each cue's time.
Enter or Space on a timeSeeks the video to that cue and keeps playing.
Escape in searchClears the search and shows every cue.

Focus: seeking leaves focus on the time button; it does not move to the player. The list does not auto-scroll while focus is inside it.

Labelling: each time's name says what it does ("Play from 12:34"). The list is named after the video.

Motion: following playback scrolls the list smoothly; under reduced motion it jumps.

Touch: each time is at least 44px tall; the hit area spans the cue's full height.

Content

Times are m:ss, matching the player. Cues are the speaker's words as spoken, in sentences, without speaker labels unless more than one person speaks. The count reads 2 matches, 1 match or No matches for “liturgy” in this video.
