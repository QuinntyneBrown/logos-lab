Lesson layout

The frame of every lesson page: the course outline beside the lesson from 1024px, the lesson's head, its body, and a foot with previous, next and Mark as complete.

Overview

The lesson layout frames a single lesson of a course: a reading lesson, a video lesson or a learning module. It keeps the course outline in a sticky aside on wide screens and behind an Outline button below 1024px, so a learner always knows where they are. The foot moves them on. The lesson content itself is prose, a player or both. For a course's landing page use the page header and the outline in the main column instead.

Used in 1 mock state.

Anatomy

  1. Lessondiv.lesson, one column below 1024px; from 1024px an aside column of --layout-aside-width and the main column.
  2. Asideaside.lesson__aside, sticky from 1024px, holding the course title (__aside-title), a progress bar and the compact outline. Hidden below 1024px.
  3. Outline button__outline-btn, a quiet button that opens the outline in a drawer; shown only below 1024px.
  4. Main__main, the lesson column.
  5. Head__head, the breadcrumb, the lesson title (__title) and meta (__meta): section, length, type.
  6. Bodythe lesson content: .prose, a player, or both.
  7. Foot__foot, previous and next links (__nav) and the completion control (__complete).

Sizes

the layout fills the page container; the aside width is --layout-aside-width.

States

The states are on the completion control. Hover and focus are the button's own. Loading shows the spinner while the completion saves. Completed replaces the button with a success status and a quiet Mark as not complete text button, and the outline ticks the lesson.

Responsive behaviour

Below 1024px the aside is hidden and the Outline button opens the outline in a drawer; the lesson is one column. From 1024px the aside is a sticky column that scrolls on its own and the button disappears. The foot wraps: at 360px the navigation sits above the completion control.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: the aside is an aside landmark named "Course outline"; previous and next sit in a nav named "Lesson". The lesson title is the page's h1.

KeyEffect
TabMoves through the outline, the lesson content, then previous, next and Mark as complete.
EnterFollows previous or next, or opens the Outline drawer.
SpaceMarks the lesson complete.

Focus: after Mark as complete, focus stays on the completion area and the status is announced; it does not jump to the next lesson.

Labelling: Previous and Next carry the lesson title as visually hidden text ("Next: Representatives, not replicas").

Motion: none of its own; the drawer follows the drawer's motion rules.

Touch: the foot buttons are full-size 44px pills, spaced apart.

Content

The title is the lesson title alone; the meta gives Section 3 · Lesson 2 and length (14 min read, Video · 24:18). The completion button reads Mark as complete; after it, Completed. On the last lesson say Complete the course.
