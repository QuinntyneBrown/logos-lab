Consent banner

The cookie consent card on a first visit: one plain sentence and three equal choices, Accept all, Reject non-essential and Customize.

Overview

The consent banner asks a visitor once whether Logos Lab may use non-essential cookies (analytics). It floats over the bottom of the page until they choose, and it does not block reading. The three choices have equal prominence (L2-115): the same quiet button style, the same size, so rejecting is as easy as accepting. Customize opens the choices in place. For product announcements use an alert; for a confirmation use a toast.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Bannerdiv.consent, a raised card fixed to the bottom of the viewport, 12px from the edges on a phone and a --size-consent card at the bottom right from 768px. consent--inline puts it in flow (settings page, documentation).
  2. Title__title, "Cookies on Logos Lab".
  3. Text__text, one or two sentences and a link to the privacy policy.
  4. Actions__actions, three quiet buttons that share the row equally.

Sizes

one size; --size-consent wide from 768px, full width less the margins below that.

States

Hover and focus are the buttons' own. The three buttons never change weight relative to each other: none is primary, none is a text link. After a choice the banner goes away and does not return until the policy changes; the choice can be changed later in Privacy and messaging settings.

Responsive behaviour

Under 768px the banner spans the bottom of the screen with a 12px margin and the three buttons wrap to fill the row, each at least as wide as its label. From 768px it is a card at the bottom right. Page content stays scrollable beneath it at every width.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: a region named by its title. It is not a modal: focus is not trapped and the page stays usable. It comes first in the tab order after the skip link so keyboard users meet it early.

KeyEffect
TabMoves through the privacy link and the three buttons.
Enter or SpaceMakes the choice and closes the banner.

Focus: after a choice, focus moves to the skip link (or the top of the page), never lost to the body.

Labelling: button labels are full phrases; the switches in Customize each have a label and a description.

Motion: the banner rises in once; under reduced motion it appears without movement.

Touch: buttons are full-size 44px pills that share the row.

Content

Say what cookies do in plain words, without legal language: We use essential cookies to keep you signed in. Name the choices as actions: Accept all, Reject non-essential, Customize. Never use "Got it", "Maybe later" or guilt ("Help us improve!").
