Popover

A small raised panel anchored to the control that opened it, for a short explanation or one light choice that does not block the page.

Overview

Use a popover for a few lines of help, a quick share or rename, or a preview of a person — anything people may glance at and dismiss without losing their place. It never blocks the page and closes when focus or a click moves elsewhere. Use a tooltip for a short label on hover or focus with nothing to click, a menu for a list of commands, and a dialog when the choice must be made before anything else.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Triggerthe button that opens it, with aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  2. Panela raised surface, 18rem wide, with a large radius and --shadow-3 (.popover).
  3. Titlea short heading in the h4 style (.popover__title) that names the panel.
  4. Bodyone or two sentences in muted small text (.popover__body).
  5. Actionsoptional small buttons, right-aligned, primary last (.popover__actions).

Sizes

one width, --layout-card-min (18rem) or the space available, whichever is smaller; height follows the content.

States

Default is open, with the trigger marked aria-expanded="true". Hover and focus show on the trigger as on any button; focus inside the panel uses the same sage ring. Closed leaves only the trigger, with aria-expanded="false"; nothing of the panel stays in the layout.

Responsive behaviour

From 768px the popover opens below its trigger and flips above when there is no room (position-try-fallbacks: flip-block). At 360px it is never wider than the screen less the page margins; when a popover would hold more than a sentence and two buttons on a phone, use a bottom sheet instead.

Theming

The panel uses --color-bg-surface-raised, a hairline --color-border-default and --shadow-3. In dark mode the raised surface is lighter than the page, and the hairline keeps the edge crisp where the shadow fades.

Accessibility

Role: a non-modal dialog (role="dialog", labelled by its title) when it holds controls. In production use the popover attribute and popovertarget on the trigger, which give light dismiss and top-layer stacking for free. The trigger carries aria-expanded and aria-controls.

KeyEffect
Enter, SpaceOn the trigger, opens or closes the popover.
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the panel and through its controls; tabbing past the last control closes it.
EscapeCloses the popover and returns focus to the trigger.

Focus: opening moves focus to the first control in the panel, or keeps it on the trigger when the panel is text only. Closing — by Escape, an action or a click outside — returns focus to the trigger.

Labelling: icon-only triggers need aria-label (About sync). The person's photo is decorative because their name is the title.

Motion: none beyond the shared colour transitions; the panel appears in place.

Touch: explainers open on tap, never on hover. Actions are btn--sm (36px) and sit --space-2 apart.

Content

Title with the object: Share Harbour Notes, Rename notebook. Keep the body to one or two sentences that answer the question the trigger raises. Action labels are a verb and an object: Copy link, Invite people, Save name.

Do and don’t

Tokens

Popovers read the raised surface, the hairline border, --shadow-3, --radius-lg, --space-5 padding and --layout-card-min for width.

Code

Wrap the trigger and the panel in .popover-anchor. In production add popover to .popover and popovertarget to the trigger; keep aria-expanded in sync when you open it from script.

<div class="popover-anchor">
  <button class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm" type="button" aria-expanded="true" aria-controls="s1253-pop-share"><svg class="icon" viewbox="0 0 24 24" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M10 14a4 4 0 0 0 5.7 0l3-3a4 4 0 0 0-5.7-5.7l-1 1"></path><path d="M14 10a4 4 0 0 0-5.7 0l-3 3a4 4 0 0 0 5.7 5.7l1-1"></path></svg>Share</button>
  <div class="popover" id="s1253-pop-share" role="dialog" aria-labelledby="s1253-pop-share-title">
    <p class="popover__title" id="s1253-pop-share-title">Share Harbour Notes</p>
    <p class="popover__body">Everyone in Northwind can read it. Invite people to let them edit.</p>
    <div class="popover__actions"><button class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm" type="button">Copy link</button><button class="btn btn--primary btn--sm" type="button">Invite people</button></div>
  </div>
</div>
