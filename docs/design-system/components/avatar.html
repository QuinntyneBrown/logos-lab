Avatar

Put a face or initials beside a name. A softly rounded square photo, never a circle, with presence shown in words.

Overview

Use an avatar to help people recognise each other at a glance: in a list, a comment, a card or the account button in the top bar. The photo is a small, softly rounded square — the kit never crops faces into circles. When there is no photo, show initials on one of the four warm tiles. An avatar never replaces a name: put the name beside it, or give it one. For a person with their role and an action, use the person list.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Portraita square photo cropped with object-fit: cover, --radius-md corners, 40px by default.
  2. Initials tilethe fallback: one or two letters on a sage, clay, fjord or oat tile, announced as an image with the person's name.
  3. Presenceoptional: a dot and a word (Online, Last active 2 hours ago). The word is the signal; the dot only repeats it.
  4. Overflow countin a group, "+4 more" after the last face.

Sizes

sm (32px) for groups, dense rows and the account button; the default (40px, no class) for lists and comments; md (48px) for cards and message threads; lg (64px) for a profile header or person card. Initials scale with the tile: the lg tile switches to the light display face. Never stretch an avatar past 96px — use a full photo instead.

States

An avatar is passive: it has no hover, focus or disabled state of its own. When it sits inside a link or the account button, that control owns the interaction. While a photo or a person record loads, reserve the exact size with a busy tile (aria-busy="true") that shimmers softly, so nothing shifts when the photo arrives.

Responsive behaviour

Avatars keep their size at every width; they never shrink to make a row fit. At 360px a group shows at most three faces and the count; a name beside an avatar wraps under itself rather than pushing the photo out. From 1024px, profile headers may use lg.

Theming

Photos are unchanged in dark mode. The four tiles and --color-tile-fg have their own dark values, so initials keep 4.5:1 contrast on both themes. Presence reads --color-online for the dot and --color-success-fg for the word.

Accessibility

Role: an <img> with alt, or a <span role="img" aria-label="…"> for initials. A group is role="group" with an aria-label that names it and gives the full count. An avatar is not a focus stop unless it sits inside a link or button.

Focus: when an avatar is the content of a link or .icon-btn, the control shows the shared 2px focus ring.

Labelling: give the person's full name — never the file name or "avatar". When the name is visible beside it, use alt="" (or aria-hidden="true" on initials) so screen readers do not hear the name twice. A button that holds only an avatar names its action: "Account menu for Amara Osei".

Motion: the presence dot breathes over 3–4 seconds and the loading tile shimmers; both stop under reduced motion, and the word "Online" stays.

Touch: an avatar inside a control sits in a 44px target, even at sm.

Content

Initials are the first letter of the first and last name, uppercase: DR for Daniel Reyes, GL for Grace Liu. For a single name use one letter. Presence is plain and relative: Online, Last active 2 hours ago, Away until Monday — never "Offline" or "Inactive". Group counts say what they count: +4 more, and the label says Northwind team, 7 people.

Code

Use <img class="avatar"> with width and height attributes that match the size, so the page does not shift while the photo loads. Fall back to .avatar-initials with a tile--* class. Add avatar--sm, avatar--md or avatar--lg for size, and aria-busy="true" on an empty tile while loading.
