Annotation

An editor's review comment anchored to a passage: a warm highlight in the text and a note beside it that can be replied to and resolved.

Overview

Annotations are how an editor reviews a submission in the studio. Naomi Ellis selects a passage, writes a note, and the expert sees the highlight in their draft and the note in the review panel. Selecting either one makes both current. Notes are resolved, not deleted, so the history stays. For public discussion under published content use the comment component; for comparing versions use the diff.

Not used in the mocks yet.

Anatomy

  1. Highlightmark.annotation, a warm wash with a thick underline over the passage.
  2. Notesol.annotations, the review panel's list, in the order the passages appear.
  3. Noteli.annotation-note, a birch card with a warm left rule.
  4. Head__head, the reviewer's portrait, name (__who) and time.
  5. Quote__quote, the passage the note is anchored to, in italics.
  6. Text__text, the comment.
  7. Actions__actions, Reply and Resolve (or Reopen).

Sizes

notes fill the review panel; highlights follow the text.

States

Default notes have a warm left rule. Current (the note or its passage is selected) turns the note's border and the highlight to the accent and sets aria-current="true" on both. Resolved greys the note, turns its rule green, mutes its text, and leaves only a dashed underline in the text.

Responsive behaviour

From 1024px the notes sit in a panel beside the draft. Below 1024px they move into a Review comments drawer opened from the workflow bar, and selecting a highlight opens the drawer at its note. Quotes and text wrap; nothing truncates.

Theming

Every colour comes from a semantic role, so the dark theme needs no component-specific rules. A light specimen nested under a dark root resets its roles.

Accessibility

Role: the highlight is a mark; screen readers announce it as highlighted. Notes are a list named "Review comments". Current note and highlight carry aria-current="true".

KeyEffect
TabMoves through each note's Reply and Resolve.
Enter on a highlight's noteScrolls the draft to the passage and makes both current.
Alt + Down / Alt + UpMoves to the next or previous comment.

Focus: resolving a note moves focus to the next open note, or to the panel heading if none remain.

Labelling: each note repeats its passage in the quote, so the note makes sense on its own. Resolved notes say Resolved in a badge, not only by colour.

Motion: scrolling to a passage is smooth unless reduced motion is set.

Touch: Reply and Resolve are separate small pills with room between them.

Content

Notes are specific and kind: ask a question or say what would make it better. Can we cite the passage in Confessions X here? beats Source? Quote the passage exactly, in curly quotes. Times follow the relative-time rule: 10:02 am, Yesterday, 4:40 pm, Sat 3 Oct.
