Dialog

Ask for one decision or one short piece of input without leaving the page, while everything behind it waits.

Core componentContrast verified

Overview

Use a dialog for a short, focused task that has to be finished or abandoned before anything else happens: confirm a destructive action, send a message, invite someone, edit a handful of fields. Keep the page behind it intact so people return to where they were. Use a drawer for filters or detail people browse alongside the page, a popover for light, non-blocking choices, and a full page for anything longer than one screen of fields.

Kit component; mocks have not been written yet.

Anatomy

  1. Iconoptional 44px tile that sets the tone (.dialog__icon; --danger for destructive actions).
  2. Titlea question or a verb and object in the heading face (.dialog__title); it names the dialog.
  3. Close buttona 32px icon button labelled Close dialog, top right.
  4. Bodyone line of consequence in muted text, then fields, people or messages (.dialog__body); it scrolls when tall.
  5. Footera hairline, then the quiet way out first and the one primary action last (.dialog__footer).

Sizes

Three widths, set by class: dialog--sm (24rem) for a one-line question, the default (32rem) for most dialogs, and dialog--lg (40rem) for a short form with paired fields. Height follows the content up to the window height less --space-12; beyond that only the body scrolls, so the title and the footer stay put.

States

Busy: after the primary action is pressed, it keeps its label, gains a spinner and aria-busy, and the dialog stays open until the work finishes. Invalid: each problem shows under its field; a dialog with more than one field also lists the problems in a form summary at the top of the body. Failed: when the action fails, a danger alert at the top of the body says what happened and that nothing was lost; the fields keep what people typed.

Responsive behaviour

Below 640px a dialog becomes a bottom sheet: full width, rounded only at the top, rising from the bottom edge, with its height capped at the window less --space-4. From 640px it centres with --space-6 around it and rounds all four corners. Footer buttons wrap onto a second line rather than shrink; paired fields in dialog--lg stack below 768px.

Theming

The dialog sits on --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-4 over the --color-bg-backdrop scrim. In dark mode the raised surface is lighter than the page, so the dialog still reads as lifted; the danger tile uses --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-icon in both themes.

Accessibility

Role: a native <dialog> opened with showModal(), which makes the rest of the page inert. Label it with its title (aria-labelledby) and describe it with its consequence sentence (aria-describedby). It follows the APG modal dialog pattern. The specimens above are static copies (role="region") so they can sit in the page.

KeyEffect
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only; focus never leaves the dialog.
EscapeCloses the dialog without acting, like Cancel.
EnterActivates the focused button, or submits the form from a field.

Focus: on open, focus moves to the first field, or to the safe button when there is no field. A destructive dialog always starts on Cancel (Keep notebook), never on the danger button. Focus is trapped inside while open and returns to the button that opened it on close. After a failed submit, focus moves to the form summary or the first invalid field.

Labelling: the close button is an icon button labelled Close dialog. The person's photo in .dialog__who is decorative because their name sits beside it. A busy primary button keeps its name and sets aria-busy.

Motion: the dialog rises --space-4 over --duration-slow with --ease-enter; under reduced motion it appears in place.

Touch: footer buttons are 44px tall; the close button is 32px, above the 24px minimum, and sits at least --space-4 from the title. Footer buttons keep --space-3 between them so a thumb cannot hit both.

Content

Title a confirmation as the question people are answering, naming the thing: Delete Harbour Notes?, Leave Field Day?. Title a task with a verb and object: Invite people to Northwind. Follow with one sentence of consequence that includes numbers when they matter: its 128 pages and every comment. Button labels repeat the verb: Delete notebook, Keep notebook — never OK and Cancel for a destructive choice. Failure messages say what did not happen and what was kept.

Code

In production use <dialog class="dialog"> with showModal(); the .backdrop wrapper is for frameworks that render their own overlay (such as the Angular CDK). Keep the header, body and footer as direct children so only the body scrolls.

<div class="dialog ds-static-dialog" role="region" aria-labelledby="s1173-dlg-publish" aria-describedby="s1173-dlg-publish-desc">
  <div class="dialog__header"><h3 class="dialog__title" id="s1173-dlg-publish">Publish the Northwind roadmap?</h3><button class="icon-btn" type="button" aria-label="Close dialog"><svg class="icon" viewbox="0 0 24 24" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M6 6l12 12M18 6 6 18"></path></svg></button></div>
  <div class="dialog__body"><p id="s1173-dlg-publish-desc">Everyone in Northwind will see the roadmap and get one notification. You can unpublish it at any time.</p></div>
  <div class="dialog__footer"><button class="btn btn--quiet" type="button">Cancel</button><button class="btn btn--primary" type="button">Publish roadmap</button></div>
</div>
