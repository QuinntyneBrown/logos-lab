Typography

Manrope for every product role, light and tight at display sizes, relaxed at body sizes; JetBrains Mono only for code.

Foundation
  • Principles
  • Specimens
  • Tokens
  • Usage
  • Sources

Principles

Manrope carries the whole product. Large headings are light (300) with tight tracking (-0.03em), which is the calm, airy signature of the look. Small headings step up to medium and semibold so they still hold their place. Body text is 16px with a relaxed 1.65 line height, and the lead paragraph is 19px at 1.85. JetBrains Mono is reserved for code, tokens and keyboard keys.

  • Use the composite roles (font: var(--text-h2)), not separate size and weight values.
  • One h1 per page. Sections step down in order, h2 then h3, with no skipped levels.
  • Display (--text-display) is for a marketing promise only, usually once per product. Accent one phrase with <em>, which turns it into the accent colour, never italic.
  • Overlines (.eyebrow, --text-overline) are uppercase with wide tracking (0.14em). They label a section; they never replace its heading.
  • Metadata is never smaller than 12px (--font-size-xs).

Usage

  • Measure: cap reading text at --layout-measure (58ch). Supporting text under a title uses --layout-measure-short (44ch).
  • Numbers: use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums (.num) wherever digits line up or change in place: stats, counts, times and tables.
  • Wrapping: headings use text-wrap: balance and paragraphs text-wrap: pretty. Never set fixed heights around labels or translated copy.
  • Fonts ship offline: assets/fonts/fonts.css declares Manrope (200–800) and JetBrains Mono (100–800) as local WOFF2 files under the SIL Open Font Licence. Applications serve the same files.
