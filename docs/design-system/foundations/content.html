Content

Warm, plain and specific — write like a thoughtful neighbour, not a marketing department.

Foundation
  • Principles
  • Specimens
  • Tokens
  • Usage
  • Sources

Principles

The voice matches the visuals: calm, warm and unhurried. Copy is specific, uses plain words and treats people as capable adults.

  • Sentence case everywhere. Headings, buttons, tabs and menu items: "Invite a teammate", not "Invite A Teammate".
  • Verbs on actions. Name the object: Save changes, Send invitation, Delete project. Never OK, Submit or Click here.
  • Specific over clever. "3 tasks due this week" beats "You're on fire!". No exclamation marks, no urgency theatre, no guilt.
  • Numbers as numerals: 3 tasks, 1,284 people, 40 km. Dates read Sat 17 Oct; times read 10:00 am, with the timezone when people are in different places.

Tokens

This foundation sets rules rather than values; the tokens it relies on live in the other foundations.

Usage

  • Keep the product's nouns stable. Use the same names in navigation, headings and buttons.
  • Write help text that answers "why are you asking?" and keep it under two lines.
  • Placeholder text is an example, never a label.
  • Names and user content can be long. Design for 40 characters and let them wrap.
  • See the Content and tone pattern for the full voice guide.
