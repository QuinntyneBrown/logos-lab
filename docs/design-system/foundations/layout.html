Layout

A centred 74rem container on a 4/8/12-column grid with margins that widen as the screen does.

Foundation
  • Principles
  • Specimens
  • Tokens
  • Usage
  • Sources

Principles

Content sits in .wrap: a centred container capped at --layout-container-max (74rem), with side margins of 20px on phones, 40px on tablets and 64px on desktops. The grid has 4 columns on phones, 8 from 48rem and 12 from 64rem. The gutter grows from 20px to 40px.

  • Reading columns stay narrow. Prose, forms and search live in --layout-narrow (44rem). Text never runs wider than its measure.
  • Two-pane layouts use named roles:
  • .two-col puts content beside an aside of --layout-aside-width.
  • .split puts a list pane of --layout-list-pane beside a detail pane.
  • .sidebar-layout puts filters or navigation in --layout-sidebar-width.
  • .settings-layout puts settings navigation in --layout-settings-nav.
  • Card grids use .grid, which places as many --layout-card-min (18rem) columns as fit.
  • The top bar is --layout-topbar-height (72px) with a hairline beneath it.

Usage

  • Compose pages from .wrap > .page-head > content blocks (.page-block, .section).
  • Collapse every multi-column layout to one column below 64rem unless the content is a short, related pair.
  • Keep z-order to the named layers in Elevation.
  • Component geometry such as dialog widths, the toast width and the textarea minimum lives in named --size-* tokens. Never write raw widths in components.
