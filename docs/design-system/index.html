Foundations

ColorOat, birch and one accent by day; warm charcoal by lamplight. Roles, not values, decide which colours touch.TypographyManrope for every product role, light and tight at display sizes, relaxed at body sizes; JetBrains Mono only for code.SpacingA 4px rhythm with a generous top end. Room is part of the calm.LayoutA centred 74rem container on a 4/8/12-column grid with margins that widen as the screen does.ElevationHairlines before boxes; four warm, very soft shadows; named layers for everything that floats.ShapeSoft corners, pill-shaped actions and small square portraits with rounded edges.MotionSlow and quiet, only when welcome — 240ms colour, 420ms movement, 640ms entrances, nothing that delays.IconographyA restrained line set on a 24-unit grid — 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in currentColor.ThemingSemantic roles carry light and dark; components never know which theme they are in.ResponsiveMobile-first at 360px, comfortable at 768px, spacious from 1024px, without losing context on the way.AccessibilityWCAG 2.2 AA is part of every component — native semantics first, visible focus, real labels, generous targets.ContentWarm, plain and specific — write like a thoughtful neighbour, not a marketing department.

Components

ButtonTrigger one action. A sage pill marks the one thing that matters; everything else stays quiet.Button groupKeep related buttons together — a row of actions, a segmented switcher for views, or a toolbar of toggles.LinkGo somewhere — an underlined sage word in a sentence, or a standalone link with an arrow that leads on.MenuA short list of actions that opens from a button. Quiet rows on a raised surface, with the destructive action last and set apart.Form fieldThe wrapper that ties a label, a control, help text and an error together, so every question is understood the first time it is read.Text fieldA single line of typed answer, from a project name to a password, plus the signature search pill.TextareaRoom for a longer answer — a project summary, meeting notes, a reply — that grows with what people write.SelectPick one answer from a short, known list — a native select with the kit's pill or field styling and a quiet chevron.CheckboxTurn one option on or off, or pick any number from a list — a soft square that fills with sage when chosen.Radio groupChoose exactly one answer from a few visible options — as calm cards with a sentence each, or as a plain list.SwitchTurn a setting on or off the moment it is touched — a sage track for on, a quiet grey track for off.SliderSet an approximate value along a range — focus hours, text size, distance — with the current value always in words beside it.File uploadAdd a file by choosing or dropping it, then see it upload, succeed or fail — with the native chooser always one key press away.Form layoutArrange fields into a calm, single-column form — sections, side-by-side pairs, an error summary and one clear submit action.Skip linkThe first thing a keyboard reaches. A dark pill that appears on focus and jumps past the top bar to the page's main content.BrandThe product's mark and lowercase wordmark. A quiet line drawing in the accent, with one warm sun dot.Top barThe bar across the top of every page. Brand on the left, a few quiet links, the person's actions on the right, and one hairline beneath.Sidebar navigationA vertical list of places inside one area of the product. Muted rows, a sage-tinted current page, and optional groups and counts.TabsSwitch between a few views of the same thing without leaving the page. Muted labels on a hairline, the selected one underlined in sage.BreadcrumbA short trail from the current page back up through its parents. Small text, quiet slashes, and the current page unlinked at the end.PaginationMove through a long list a page at a time. The signature is calm: one Show more button, a thin sage meter and a plain count.StepperShow where someone is in a short, ordered flow. A row of quiet bars that fill with sage as each step is done.FooterThe quiet end of every page. A hairline, a line about who makes the product, a few muted links and one line of fine print.Page headerThe top of a page's content. An eyebrow, a light display title and one muted line that says what is here, with the page's actions to the side.Container, grid and stackThe layout primitives every page is built from. A centred container, vertical stacks, wrapping clusters, an auto-fit grid and the page's column patterns.CardHold one person, project or gathering as a calm, rounded surface: a small square photo, a name, a few facts and one quiet action.AccordionFold secondary detail under a short question or heading, so a long page stays calm until someone asks for more.DividerA single hairline that separates groups of content when spacing alone is not enough.DialogAsk for one decision or one short piece of input without leaving the page, while everything behind it waits.Drawer and sheetA panel that slides in from an edge for filters, navigation or detail, keeping the page it belongs to in view.PopoverA small raised panel anchored to the control that opened it, for a short explanation or one light choice that does not block the page.TooltipA few words on a dark chip that appear on hover or keyboard focus, to explain a control without adding to the page.Sign-in cardThe calm, centred card for signing in, creating an account and the steps around them: one title, a few fields and one way forward.TableCompare the same few facts across many rows: a captioned, hairline table that sorts, selects and scrolls without losing its header.ListA single column of items separated by hairlines, from plain links to rich rows with a photo, two lines of text and an action.Description listPairs of small uppercase labels and plain values, for the facts about a person, project or gathering.AvatarPut a face or initials beside a name. A softly rounded square photo, never a circle, with presence shown in words.Badge and statusLabel the state of a thing in two or three words, or tag it with a topic. Quiet tinted pills; the word always carries the meaning.Chip and tagNarrow a list with one tap. A pressed chip turns sage; an applied filter shows as a chip you can remove.Stat and KPIShow a few numbers that matter, large and light, with a plain label underneath. Hairlines divide them; nothing shouts.TimelineTell what happened, or what will happen, in order. A hairline spine with small sage rings, a time and one sentence each.Code blockMonospaced text on a soft linen panel for commands, settings and snippets people read closely or copy.Alert and bannerTell people something that stays true while they are on the page. Tinted, calm, specific, and always with a way forward.ToastConfirm that something just happened without taking people away from their work. Raised, brief, and in the corner.Inline messageOne short line beside the thing it is about — Saving…, All changes saved, Couldn't save. The smallest piece of feedback.Progress barShow how far along a task is when you can measure it. A thin sage line on a hairline track, with the words above it.SpinnerSay "working on it" for a moment of unknown length. A small open ring in the text colour, always beside words.SkeletonHold the shape of content while it loads, so the page does not jump. Soft linen blocks that breathe slowly.Empty stateTurn an empty list into a next step. A sage icon tile, a plain title, one helpful sentence and the action that fills the space.Error pageWhen a whole screen cannot be shown, say so calmly, say what is safe, and offer the next step. A large quiet number, a plain title and a way out.HeroOpen a landing or welcome page with one calm promise, one sage action and a small, human photograph.Feature areaShow the few places a product offers as numbered, quiet cards that each lead somewhere.Grain panelThe signature invitation: a warm clay panel with a whisper of wood grain that asks for one considered next step.Testimonial and verseLet a real person speak: a small quote card with a portrait, or one centred line set like a verse between sections.Person listIntroduce people as calm rows of small square portraits, a name that links to them and one line of context.Search and filter toolbarSay how many results there are, let people sort them, and keep every filter in view and easy to undo.Notification itemA list of updates people can come back to: unread rows wear a sage wash and a dot, read rows fall quiet.Profile headerThe top of a person's or a thing's own page. A square portrait or tile, a large name, one quiet line of context and the actions that belong to it.CommentA short, signed reply under a piece of work. Portrait, name, when, then the words, separated from the next comment by a hairline.Message threadA private conversation between people. Soft bubbles, theirs on the left and yours on the right in sage, with a labelled reply box beneath.InboxThe list of conversations. Each row is a link with a portrait, a name, the last line and when it was sent; unread rows carry a sage dot.Media tileA captioned 4:3 tile for a screenshot or photo, or a calm illustrated placeholder when there is no image yet. Tiles sit in a two- or three-column grid.ProseSet long-form pages such as privacy, terms and help articles in a calm, readable column with a clear heading rhythm.Danger zoneFence off the actions that cannot be undone, say exactly what will be lost, and ask for the name before acting.Project cardA sample domain card for a piece of work in progress, kept as a reference. A letter mark, the name, who leads it, one line about it, a few facts and its stage.Event rowA sample domain row for a dated gathering, kept as a reference. A large day number, the title, when and where, how many are going and one action; plus the matching header for the event's own page.RSVP panelA sample domain panel for reserving a place at a gathering, kept as a reference. It says whether there is room, shows how full it is and holds the one action that fits.SuggestionA sample domain card that suggests a person to meet, kept as a reference. Who they are, a plain reason why, and two calm choices.

Patterns

FormsAsk for a clear answer, keep everything a person typed, and say what happens next.Feedback and loadingShow what is happening and what changed, close to where it happened, for as long as it matters.Empty and error statesWhen there is nothing to show, say why in one calm sentence and offer the one next step that helps.Navigation and page structureKeep every page in the same calm order, so people always know where they are and how to get back.Dialogs and overlaysAsk for one focused decision on top of the page, then return the person exactly where they were.Data tables and listsShow many things so they are easy to scan, compare and act on, at every width.NotificationsTell people what changed through the quietest channel that still reaches them in time.Content and toneWrite the way a thoughtful colleague talks: calm, warm, specific and plain.
