1.2 Sabbath for the Always-On Mind

Article · 8 min readDr. Abena MensahFree order

Most of us no longer stop working. We stop typing, perhaps, but the phone in our pocket keeps the inbox open and the assistant ready. The tools are always on, and so, slowly, are we.

The fourth commandment does not ask us to rest because we are tired. It asks us to rest because God rested, and because a people who stop one day in seven remember that the world does not depend on them (Exodus 20:8–11).

What Sabbath is for

Sabbath is not a productivity technique. It is a weekly confession that we are creatures. An always-on mind forgets that; a day without making reminds it.

“A day without making is a day for remembering who made us.”

Try this before lesson 1.3

  • Pick one tool you will not open for 24 hours this week.
  • Notice what you reach for instead, and write it down.
