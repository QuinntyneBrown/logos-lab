We've updated our Terms of Use and our Privacy Policy

Both took effect on Thu 1 Oct 2026. Read what changed, then accept to carry on to Logos Lab.

What changed

  • We now name each service that handles your data, and where. All of them process it in Canada.
  • We say how long we keep things: notifications 90 days, security logs 1 year, and deleted accounts are purged after 30 days.
  • A new section explains cohort posts: who can see them and what happens to them if you leave.
  • Analytics cookies are now off until you turn them on, and your choice lasts 12 months.
  • You can download all your data, including messages and consent records, from Settings, once every 24 hours.
  • Certificates: if you delete your account, verifying one of your certificates shows “No longer available”.
  • Experts: what we keep about content you published after your expert role ends, and how it stays attributed to you.
  • Messages between members are deleted when the sender's account is purged; we don't read them unless one is reported.
  • Contact: write to help@logoslab.ca about anything in this policy, and we'll answer within 30 days.
