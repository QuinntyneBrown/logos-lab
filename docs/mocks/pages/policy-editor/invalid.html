6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, we deactivate it straight away and erase it after 30 days.

Posts in a cohort discussion are kept for 2 years after the cohort ends, so its members can look back on what they learned together. After that they are erased.

7. Who helps us run Logos Lab

We use a small number of service providers, all bound by contract to protect your information and to store it in Canada. A full list, including the provider that measures video playback, is on this page.
