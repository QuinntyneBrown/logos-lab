Workshop: Writing an AI Policy for Your Church

Saturday 7 November 2026, 10:00 am to 12:30 pm ETHybrid · online and at St. Brigid's Hall, OttawaHosted by Rev. Dr. Thomas Hale

About

A practical morning for pastors, staff and church councils. Rev. Dr. Thomas Hale walks through the questions a good policy answers, then you draft one in small groups, with a template to take home.

Places are limited to 40 so every group gets attention.
