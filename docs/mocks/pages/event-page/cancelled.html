Faith and AI Evening: Is Creativity Sacred?

Was Thursday 22 October 2026, 7:00 to 9:00 pm ETIn person · St. Brigid's Hall, OttawaHosted by Logos Lab

About

An evening of conversation about making things: art, music, writing and code. If a machine can produce a hymn or a painting, what happens to our sense that creativity reflects the Creator? Coffee and dessert from 7:00, conversation from 7:30.

The hall is step-free, with a hearing loop. Free parking behind the building.
