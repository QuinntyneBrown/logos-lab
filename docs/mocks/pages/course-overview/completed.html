Christian Ethics for AI Builders

A practical course for engineers, designers and product people who follow Jesus and build with AI. Rev. Dr. Thomas Hale works through truthfulness, justice and care, one design decision at a time.

Your progress100%

What you’ll learn

  • Name the moral questions hidden in ordinary engineering choices
  • Practise truthfulness in interfaces, outputs and disclosure
  • Test a system for who bears its costs and who is left out
  • Recognise when care for the vulnerable means not building something
  • Write your own code of practice and keep it at work
