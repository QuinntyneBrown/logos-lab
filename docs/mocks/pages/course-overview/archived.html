Faith, Work and Automation

A short course for congregations and workplaces on what automation means for human work, rest and dignity, and how a church can walk with people through the change.

What you’ll learn

  • Describe work as part of God’s good creation, before and after the fall
  • Explain what automation changes about work, and what it can’t
  • Care for people whose work has changed or ended
  • Keep a rhythm of work and rest when tools never switch off
