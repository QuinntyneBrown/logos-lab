Description

Most bias in AI is not malice. It is memory: a model trained on yesterday's decisions will repeat them. In this exercise you play a hiring manager at a mid-sized charity and review a screening tool's shortlist.

You will meet three applicants and the reasons the tool ranked them. Some reasons are sound. Some are proxies for postcode, age or a gap in work history, and every score in this exercise comes from a real hiring pattern.

At the end you decide what to keep, what to question and what to refuse, and you see how each choice scores.
