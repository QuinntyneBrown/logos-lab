About Dr. Abena Mensah

Abena Mensah is a theologian who writes about what it means to be human. Dr. Mensah's work in theological anthropology asks how the church's teaching that we are made in the image of God should shape the way we build and use machines that speak and reason.

Dr. Mensah teaches systematic theology, leads the Imago Dei cohort on Logos Lab each autumn, and preaches regularly in congregations across the Maritimes and Ontario. The book Made, Not Manufactured came out in 2025.

Areas of expertise

  • The image of God and personhood
  • Theology of technology
  • Formation and education
