Augustine and the Algorithm

Dr. Lucía Ortega · Published 12 Oct 2026

0:00 / 31:05

About this video

Augustine wrote that we become what we love. Dr. Lucía Ortega reads Book Ten of the Confessions alongside the feeds and recommendation systems that shape our attention, and asks what a well-ordered love might look like online.

Recorded for the Logos Lab autumn series. Captions are available in English and French.

Transcript

Select a time to play from there.

  1. Augustine opens the Confessions with a sentence about restlessness.

  2. Our heart is restless until it rests in you. He meant it as a confession, not a slogan.

  3. Sixteen centuries later, we carry devices designed to keep that restlessness going.

  4. A recommendation system does not hate us. It simply learns what holds our attention.

  5. Augustine would call that a question about love: what are we being taught to love?

  6. In Book Ten he describes memory as a vast palace, full of rooms we did not choose to fill.

  7. Tonight I want to read that passage alongside the feeds that now fill those rooms for us.
