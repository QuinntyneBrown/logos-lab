Can a Machine Pray?

Prof. Simon Keller · Published 21 Sep 2026

Prayer is not a transaction of words. It is attention turned toward someone.

12:34 / 24:18

About this video

Software can now produce fluent prayers on request. Prof. Simon Keller asks what prayer is, whether attention can be simulated, and why the answer matters for how churches use these tools.

  • Theology of technology

Transcript

2 matches

  1. Last week a student asked me whether a chatbot could say the Lord's Prayer.

  2. Of course it can say it. The harder question is whether it can pray it.

  3. So the question is not whether the words are right.

  4. A machine can produce a flawless psalm. Many already do.

  5. Prayer is not a transaction of words. It is attention turned toward someone.

  6. And attention is the first gift we give to anyone, human or divine.

  7. Simone Weil said that attention, taken to its highest degree, is the same thing as prayer.
