Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed Mon 5 Oct 2026.

Logos Lab is for everyone who wants to think carefully about faith and artificial intelligence, including people who use a screen reader, magnification, voice control, captions or only a keyboard. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across the whole site.

How we check

  • Every page and every state is scanned automatically with axe-core before each release, and a release with a serious or critical issue does not ship.
  • New and changed screens are tested by hand with NVDA and Chrome or Firefox on Windows, VoiceOver and Safari on iOS, and with a keyboard alone.
  • Pages adapt from small phones to wide screens, and reading text stays at a comfortable line length.
  • Light and dark themes both meet the contrast minimums, and we respect your device's reduced-motion setting.

Known limitations

We know about these gaps and are working on them:

  • Some learning modules are interactive packages built outside Logos Lab. Where a module is not fully accessible, its page says so and offers a text alternative.
  • Captions on older videos were generated automatically and are being reviewed by people. Until then, they may contain mistakes, especially with names and Scripture references.
  • Downloaded certificates are tagged PDFs, but the decorative border is not yet marked as an artifact in every PDF reader.

Tell us what isn't working

If something on Logos Lab is hard to use, we want to hear about it. Write to help@logoslab.ca with the page address and what happened. A person reads every message, and we reply within two working days.

If you need a course, article or transcript in another format, ask us at the same address and we'll do our best to provide it.
