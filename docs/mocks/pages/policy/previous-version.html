Who we are

Logos Lab is an expert-curated place to learn where Christian faith meets artificial intelligence. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, where it's kept and the choices you have.

What we collect and why

  • Your name and email, to sign you in, put your name on comments and certificates, and send account emails.
  • Your learning, such as enrolments, progress, scores and certificates, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • What you post, such as comments, cohort posts and messages, so others can read them.
  • Security records, such as sign-in times and IP addresses, to protect your account.

Where your data is kept

Everything is stored and processed in Canada.

We use cloud hosting and an email service. Both store your data in Canada.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember your choices. Analytics and video-quality cookies are off until you turn them on in Cookie settings.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data from Settings, once every 24 hours.
  • Delete your account. You have 30 days to change your mind.
  • Unsubscribe from Platform news with one click in any email.

Questions

Write to help@logoslab.ca. We answer privacy questions within 30 days.
