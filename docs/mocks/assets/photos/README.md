# Photography

Stock photographs from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash Licence (free to use, no attribution
required), resized and recompressed (scene 1600 px, portraits 480 px square). They stand in for the
people and places of the product; production must use consented photographs. Portraits are named after
the kit's fictional cast (see the skill's `references/kit-catalog.md`); the people pictured are not those
characters. An app may reuse them for its own cast.
