Our community guidelines

Before your first comment, cohort post or message, please read and accept how we talk with one another here. Your comment is posted as soon as you accept.

Version 3 · updated 1 Oct 2026

1. Speak as you would in a church hall

Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume the best of others, ask before you assume, and leave room for people still working things out.

2. Stay on the subject

Keep comments to the piece or the cohort you are in. Questions are welcome; promotion of products, services or fundraising is not.

3. Honour other traditions

Logos Lab brings together Christians from many traditions. Make your case from Scripture, reason and experience without mocking anyone's church.

4. Be careful with claims about AI

Say where a claim comes from. If you are speculating about what models can or will do, say so.

5. Protect people's privacy

Don't share anyone's personal details, private messages or pastoral situations without their permission, including your congregation's.

6. Report, don't retaliate

If something breaks these guidelines, report it. Moderators review every report, usually within 24 hours, and never tell anyone who reported them.

Breaking these guidelines can lead to a comment being hidden or removed, a warning, a suspension or, for serious or repeated harm, a ban. You'll always be told why, and you can appeal.
