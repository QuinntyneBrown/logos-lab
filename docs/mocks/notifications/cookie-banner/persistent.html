What Augustine Might Say About Large Language Models

Dr. Lucía Ortega · 29 Sep 2026 · updated 6 Oct 2026

Augustine never met a machine that could finish his sentences, but he wrote at length about memory, attention and the restless heart. Read beside today's language models, the Confessions asks a sharper question than whether machines can think: what happens to us when we let them remember for us?
