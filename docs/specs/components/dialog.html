Our community guidelines

Before your first comment, cohort post or message, please read and accept how we talk with one another here. Your comment is posted as soon as you accept.

Version 3 · updated 1 Oct 2026

1. Be kind and curious

Assume the other person is trying to understand, not to win. Ask before you argue.

2. Disagree about ideas, not people

You may think a view is wrong. You may not attack the person who holds it.

3. Say where claims come from

If you are speculating about what models can or will do, say so.

4. Be careful with claims about AI

Link to your source. Quote accurately, and mark what you have added.

5. Protect people's privacy

Don't share anyone's personal details, private messages or pastoral situations without their permission.

6. What happens when guidelines are broken

A comment may be hidden or removed, followed by a warning, a suspension or a ban. You'll always be told why, and you can appeal.

Responsive

Below 640 px the dialog is a bottom sheet (320 and 360 px frames); from 640 px it is centred with all four corners rounded (768 px frame). Footer buttons wrap onto a second line rather than shrink.
