Responsive

The 320 px frame shows the event time zone toggle in English and in the pseudo-locale: the second wraps each label inside its segment and stays in the column (D-2). The 360 px frame shows the 13-button toolbar wrapping onto further rows. The 375 px frame shows the preview banner moving the three-segment group to its own line as a whole. At 768 px the analytics bar keeps the group on one row beside the switch.

Motion: pressing changes the fill and edge at once under reduced motion, and the pressed surface never slides.

AC-34

Performance: the group has a fixed size from first paint, and every button reserves its hairline edge, so pressing shifts nothing.
