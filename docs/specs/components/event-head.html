Event head

The top of an event's own page: the date block, the title as the page's h1, three facts, a slot for the time zone toggle and the page's actions. Requirements: event-head.md. Design system: event-row (its "Header" example).

Variants

The loading photo's place in the date column from 48rem, the line breaking of long words and the wrapping toggle labels are required (D-5, D-6, D-13); they are drawn with a specimen-only style until the design system adds them. The empty actions row is not drawn (D-4).
