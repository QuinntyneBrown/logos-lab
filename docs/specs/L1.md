# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Logos Lab ([www.logoslab.ca](https://www.logoslab.ca)) is an expert-curated learning platform centred
on the intersection of Christianity and AI. These requirements describe what the platform must do.
`L2.md` holds the detailed, testable requirements that trace to each L1.

## Scope decisions

- **Free at launch.** All content is free with an account. Payments, subscriptions and expert payouts
  are out of scope until a later requirement adds them.
- **Experts author, Logos Lab reviews.** Experts are invited, write content in the platform, and
  Logos Lab editors approve it before it is published. Logos Lab also publishes its own content.
- **Community** means comments on content, course cohorts and direct messages. Standalone topic forums
  are out of scope.
- **English only, i18n-ready.** The platform launches in English (en-CA). It is built so French can be
  added without rework.
- **Production-grade, security-first, responsive (XS to XL), light and dark themes, WCAG 2.2 AA.**

## Roles

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Visitor | Anyone who is not signed in |
| Learner | Any signed-in member. Every account has this role. |
| Expert | An invited subject-matter expert who authors content and leads cohorts |
| Editor | Logos Lab staff who review, publish and curate content |
| Moderator | Logos Lab staff who handle reports and enforce community standards |
| Administrator | Logos Lab staff who manage users, roles and site settings |

## L1-001: Discovery and search
Visitors and members must be able to browse, filter and search all published content (courses, learning
modules, videos, articles, curated collections, events and expert profiles) from a home page, a catalogue
and a site-wide search.

## L1-002: Accounts and authentication
People must be able to create an account, verify their email address, sign in and out, recover access
when they forget their password, and manage their own profile and account settings.

## L1-003: Roles and authorization
The platform must support the roles Visitor, Learner, Expert, Editor, Moderator and Administrator. Each
role must be able to see and do only what it is permitted to, and a user's own data must never be
exposed to another user.

## L1-004: Expert profiles
Each expert must have a public profile that presents who they are and everything they have contributed
to Logos Lab. Members must be able to follow an expert.

## L1-005: Courses
Learners must be able to enrol in structured courses, which are written by experts or by Logos Lab and
made of ordered sections and lessons. Learners must be able to progress through a course, see their
progress, complete it, and receive a verifiable certificate of completion.

## L1-006: Learning modules
Learners must be able to launch interactive SCORM learning modules in the Tessera scorm-player, both on
their own and as course lessons. The platform must record their progress, completion and score, and
must let them resume where they stopped.

## L1-007: Videos
Learners must be able to watch videos in the Tessera video-player with adaptive quality, captions and a
transcript, and must be able to resume where they stopped.

## L1-008: Articles
Anyone must be able to read published long-form articles that carry author attribution, reading time
and links to related content.

## L1-009: Curated collections
Experts and Logos Lab must be able to group content of any type into themed, ordered collections.
Members must be able to view and follow them.

## L1-010: Events
Users must be able to discover upcoming online and in-person events, register or join a waitlist,
add events to their calendar, receive reminders, and watch recordings of past events.

## L1-011: Community
Members must be able to discuss content in comments, take part in private course cohorts, and exchange
direct messages, all within enforced community standards.

## L1-012: Learner dashboard
Signed-in learners must have a personal space that shows their in-progress and completed learning,
saved items, followed experts and collections, registered events, and certificates.

## L1-013: Content authoring and publishing
Experts and editors must be able to create, edit, preview, submit, review, schedule, publish, revise and
archive content through an editorial workflow. No expert content is published without editor approval.

## L1-014: Moderation and trust and safety
Members must be able to report content and behaviour. Moderators must be able to review reports and act
on them, and every action must be recorded in an audit trail.

## L1-015: Notifications
The platform must notify users in-app and by email about the activity they care about. Users must
control their notification preferences, and email must comply with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation
(CASL).

## L1-016: Administration
Administrators must be able to manage users, roles, expert invitations, the topic taxonomy, featured
content and site settings.

## L1-017: Reporting and analytics
Experts, editors and administrators must be able to see engagement and completion metrics for the
content they are responsible for, without exposing individual learners' personal data unnecessarily.

## L1-018: Security
The platform must protect user accounts, content and infrastructure against the OWASP Top 10 and
must secure all data in transit and at rest.

## L1-019: Privacy and data protection
The platform must comply with PIPEDA. It must keep personal data in Canada, collect only what it needs
with informed consent, and let users export or delete their personal data.

## L1-020: Performance and scalability
Pages and APIs must meet defined response-time and Core Web Vitals budgets, and must keep meeting them
under expected peak load.

## L1-021: Accessibility
Every page, dialog, player and notification must conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

## L1-022: Responsive design and theming
Every screen must work across extra-small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium (>=768px), large (>=992px)
and extra-large (>=1200px) viewports, in both the light and the dark theme.

## L1-023: Reliability, observability and operations
The platform must be highly available on AWS. It must be monitored, logged, backed up and recoverable,
and it must be deployed and migrated in a controlled, repeatable way.

## L1-024: SEO and social sharing
Public content must be discoverable by search engines and must show rich previews when shared on
social platforms and in messaging apps.

## L1-025: Internationalization readiness
The platform must launch in English and must be built so that additional languages (French first) can
be added without changing application code.
